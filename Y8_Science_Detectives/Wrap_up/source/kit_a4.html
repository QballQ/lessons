Case closed: who took the notebook?结案：谁拿走了笔记本？

Year 8 Science Detectives wrap-up lesson, 40 minutes. Groups put all the evidence together on one board, open a new piece of evidence, give a verdict, work out where the notebook is, and find it in the freezer.

Each group's kit

Envelope 1 (open at the start): 4 suspect cards, 5 lab report cards, sign-in sheet. Envelope 2 (sealed): cleaning log. Envelope 3 (sealed): Where is the notebook? Also on the table: the A3 evidence board, 1 verdict slip, pencils. Students can use their own case files and evidence logs as well.

The frozen notebook (the day before)

  1. Glue the notebook cover (page 11) on a blue notebook or exercise book. Optional: write a few "snowflake crystal" notes inside.
  2. Seal it in a zip-lock freezer bag with the "Evidence" label. Put it in the lab freezer behind the ice-cube trays, at least 2 hours before the lesson so the bag is frosty.
  3. No freezer near the room? Use a cool box with ice packs and a paper sign "LAB FREEZER 实验室冰柜".
  4. If you teach two classes, put the notebook back in the freezer between lessons.

What the class should find

  • Step 2: nobody matches all five clues. Dr Huang matches four, but the puddle does not fit her (vinegar is an acid). Ask: "Do we ignore the clue that does not fit? Or do we find out why?"
  • Step 3: the cleaning log shows the floor by the sink was mopped with soapy cleaner at 6:30 pm. The notebook was still on the desk at 6:50 pm. So the puddle was there before the notebook went missing. It is mop water: contaminated evidence. Soap is an alkali, which explains the blue-green result. Set clue 4 aside. Nobody did anything wrong.
  • Step 4: Dr Huang is the only suspect who matches all four reliable clues. No single clue shows it; the combination does.
  • Step 5: the notebook was last seen at 6:50 pm and missing at 7:30 pm. Chef Zhou left at 6:20 pm. Max left at 7:00 pm. Dr Huang was in the lab from 7:00 to 7:20 pm. Lily was in the prep room and heard someone in the lab at about 7:10 pm.
  • Step 7: the note says "safe and cold". The footprint at G5 points towards the lab freezer at J4 and J5. The notebook is in the freezer.

The truth

Dr Ruby Huang took the notebook at about 7:05 pm to read the snowflake method, because her own demonstration was going wrong. She heard a noise, panicked and hid it in the freezer in a freezer bag. She wrote the note with her brand B pen so that Professor Frost would not worry, and spilled her baking soda on the way to the freezer.

If the students' own results were different

The lab report cards give the confirmed results, so every group works from the same evidence even if a test did not work for them. Say: "Real labs repeat tests and compare results. These are the results the lab confirmed."

If a group's verdict is wrong

Praise the reasoning that was good, then go back to the board: "Which clue did we trust too much? Which clue did we ignore?" The reveal is a learning moment, not a test.

Language

Chinese talk is welcome in every step. Students need only three English frames: "___ matches ___ of 4 clues." "We think ___ took the notebook." "The notebook is in the ___."

What really happened真相

The next morning, Professor Frost opened the lab freezer to get ice for the Fair. Behind the ice-cube trays was a freezer bag. Inside was her blue notebook: cold, a little frosty, but every page safe.概要：第二天早上，弗罗斯特教授在实验室冰柜里找到了完好的笔记本。

Dr Ruby Huang came to see her before the Fair opened. "I am so sorry," she said. "My demonstration was going wrong, and I wanted to read your snowflake method. I heard a noise, I panicked, and I hid the notebook. I wrote the note so you would not worry. I spilled my baking soda on the way to the freezer."概要：黄若琳来道歉：她的演示出了问题，想看雪花方法；听到声音慌了，就把笔记本藏了起来。纸条是她写的，小苏打是她路上洒的。

Professor Frost was quiet for a moment. Then she smiled. "You could have just asked," she said. "Science works better when we share. Why don't we present the snowflake demonstration together?"概要：弗罗斯特教授笑着说：分享让科学做得更好，我们一起做演示吧。

That afternoon, two scientists stood side by side at the Winter Science Fair, and the snowflakes were perfect.概要：那天下午，两位科学家并肩站在冬季科学展上，雪花完美无瑕。
