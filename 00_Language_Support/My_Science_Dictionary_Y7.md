# My Science Dictionary | 我的科学词典: Year 7 Ice Engineers

Series: Ice Engineers 冰雪工程师 | A5 booklet, one per student, used every week | Weeks 1 to 10
Chinese text status: draft, needs native-speaker check

---

## 0. How to use (for the teacher)

- Hand the booklet out in Week 1 together with the Scientist's Logbook 科学日志. Students write their name and class on the cover. Keep the 4 spares with the logbook spares for students who join late or lose theirs.
- When a key word comes up in a lesson, point to its page. Say the English word; a student reads the Chinese and the Chinese meaning aloud. This is the "Point to the dictionary" routine in Teacher Guide Section 4 and Language Access Policy Section 9.
- In the last minute of Think and Reflect 思辨与反思, when there is time, students write their own example on the "My example 我的例子" line for one or two of that day's words. An example can be a sentence, a phrase or a drawing with a label.
- A Band 1 student may write the example in Chinese. Praise the English key word, accept the Chinese sentence. Do not correct spelling in the booklet; it is the student's own record.
- The booklet stays open on the desk during every lesson and during the Week 5 checkpoint 阶段检测. It is closed and put away during the skills check 技能检测 in Weeks 1 and 10, because the same paper is used both times and the two scores are compared.
- The fold-under word bank 折叠词库 on each worksheet carries the same words with the same Chinese, so students meet one pairing everywhere. The word bank is for that lesson; the booklet is the long-term version that collects all ten weeks.
- Words are listed in the week where they are first taught. A word that comes back in a later week is not printed again; the week section ends with "Also this week 本周还会用到" and the week where the word lives, so students learn to turn back.
- The booklet is the student's to keep. It goes home at the end of Week 10 with the certificate. The back cover, "Words I found by myself 我自己找到的词", is for words students meet outside the key words tables; there is no mark for it.

## 1. Booklet [Paste into Claude Design]

Create an A5 portrait, black-and-white-printable booklet for Year 7, "My Science Dictionary 我的科学词典", using the Think Like a Scientist design system, Ice Engineers series.

Print spec: A5 portrait (148 mm by 210 mm), saddle-stitched, printed on A4 sheets folded once and stapled twice on the spine. The page count must be a multiple of 4; the expected count is 24 pages (cover, inside cover, 18 pages of week sections, 2 pages of index, inside back cover, back cover). If the entries need more room, go to 28 pages rather than shrinking the text or cutting entries. 90 gsm white paper throughout, printed in black and white; the design must work in greyscale, with the series told apart by its ice crystal icon and pattern, not by colour. Copies: one per student plus 4 spares per class. Body text at least 11 pt for English and 10.5 pt for Chinese; English primary, Chinese beneath in the lighter weight, as in the rest of the design system. Page numbers at the outer foot of every page except the cover and back cover.

**Cover**: title "My Science Dictionary" with "我的科学词典" beneath it in the lighter weight. Ice Engineers series icon (ice crystal) and the series name "Ice Engineers 冰雪工程师". Programme wordmark "Think Like a Scientist | 像科学家一样思考" at the foot. Two fields with ruled lines: "Name 姓名: ______________" and "Class 班级: ______________". One line beneath the fields: "Think in any language. Science words in English. 思考可用任何语言，科学词汇用英语。"

**Inside cover: How to use this book 怎样使用这本词典**

Four numbered lines, each English sentence followed by its Chinese on the next line in the lighter weight, with a small icon from the instruction words set beside each number (Look, Read, Listen or speak, Write):

1. Find this week's page. Each week has its own section. 找到本周的页面。每周都有自己的部分。
2. Read the English meaning, then the Chinese meaning. 先读英文意思，再读中文意思。
3. Say the English word out loud. Point to the picture. 大声说出这个英语单词。指一指图。
4. Write your own example on the line. You may write it in Chinese. 在横线上写下你自己的例子。可以用中文写。

Beneath the four lines, one short reminder box: "Words you meet again are not printed twice. Look for Also this week 本周还会用到 and turn back to that week. 再次出现的词不重复印。看到“本周还会用到”，就翻回那一周。"

**Week sections (one per week, Weeks 1 to 10)**

Each week section starts with a heading band: "Week n 第n周 | [English title] / [中文标题]", with the ice crystal icon at the right of the band. The English title is primary; the Chinese title sits beneath it in the lighter weight.

Every entry in every week uses exactly the same layout, which is described here once and then given as data in the tables below:

- Line 1: the **English word** in bold, with the **中文** term immediately beneath it in the lighter weight. To the right of the word, a tiny line icon about 1.5 cm square, drawn from the entry's Picture cue 图. Icons are single-weight line drawings, no fills except where the cue says shaded, so they print cleanly in black and white.
- Line 2: "Meaning 意思" label, then the English meaning in one line (15 words or fewer).
- Line 3: the Chinese meaning (意思 中文) in the lighter weight, 20 characters or fewer.
- Line 4: one ruled writing line labelled "My example 我的例子: ______________". The line runs the full width of the entry.
- A thin rule separates entries. Fit 4 to 5 entries per A5 page; never split an entry across two pages.
- Where a week section ends with "Also this week 本周还会用到: ...", set it as one line in the lighter weight at the foot of that week's last entry page. Each item is the English word, its Chinese and the week in brackets, so students know where to turn back.

The data for every entry follows. Column headings: English | 中文 | Meaning 意思 (English) | 意思 (中文) | Picture cue 图. The sixth element, "My example 我的例子: ______________", goes under every entry and is not repeated in the tables.
