## How to use this glossary

- Every file uses exactly these pairings. If a native speaker changes a term, change it here first, then in every file.
- Part 1 is the key words taught in lessons (from the Key words tables and vocabulary panels). Part 2 is fixed labels that appear on templates, slides and worksheets. Part 3 is instruction words. Part 4 is the sentence frames with their Chinese. Part 5 is for terms added during the language pass.
- Student-friendly meanings are in simple English; the dictionary booklets add the Chinese meaning.
