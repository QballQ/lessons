# Language access audit | 语言支持审查: Years 7 and 8

Think Like a Scientist | Review of translation coverage and the extras put in place | October 2026
Chinese text status: draft, needs native-speaker check

---

## 1. The question

Feedback from the Year 7 and Year 8 classes was that students could not tell what a lesson was for or what a task was asking. The classes range from nearly no English to conversational English. This review asked, for every Year 7 and Year 8 file: what already carried Chinese, what needed more, what did not, and what extras were missing. Year 9 was not reviewed.

## 2. What the review found

**What was already bilingual, and stayed as it was.** Lesson and slide titles, the five phase tags, every vocabulary panel, the Key words table in each lesson plan, the safety slide and kit card hazards, the Year 8 case file printables (cover, briefing headings, scene map labels, suspect card field names, evidence log), the Year 7 fold-under word banks, the skills check and checkpoint question stems (gloss lines), the interest survey, and every certificate, report and feedback slip. On a typical lesson file about a third of the slide lines and a fifth of the worksheet lines carried Chinese, almost all of it vocabulary.

**What was missing, in every file.** The frame of the lesson had no Chinese at all:
- the learning goals (they appeared only in the teacher's lesson plan, never on a slide or a worksheet);
- the instructions: what to do on each Do Now, Do and Think slide, and every worksheet task line ("Fill the gaps using the word bank", "Draw a line from each picture to the right box");
- the questions students had to answer: exit tickets, Think prompts, Core, Stretch and Challenge stems, the My thinking question;
- the sentence frames (students were asked to use English frames whose meaning was not shown);
- table headings, field labels and the inline word banks (the Year 8 word banks were English only);
- the clean-up lines on kit cards (students do the clean-up);
- the reading texts on cards and case files (statements, puzzle prompts), which had no Chinese way in at all.

Across the ten Year 7 files alone the auditors counted about 630 sentences to pair, 28 key idea lines, 39 gist lines, 82 word bank items, 101 frame lines and 540 labels. Every file had Core tasks that a student with almost no English could not do even with the pictures and word bank, because the task relied on reading an English cloze, English picture labels or an English scenario (for example the Week 3 exit ticket about a bouncing ball, with no picture on the strip and none of its words in any word bank).

**What did not need translation, and why.** The lesson plan body, speaker notes, answer keys, kit lists, teacher prep, mark schemes and notes for the incoming teacher are for an English-speaking teacher and stay English. Design instructions in the paste blocks ("Visual: ...") are for Claude Design, not students. Numbers, units, names and letters need no translation. The English explanation on Learn slides and the English reading texts stay as they are: students hear the teacher, see the picture and have the vocabulary panel, and reading some English science is part of the programme; what they gain is one Chinese key idea or gist line, not a sentence-by-sentence translation, so the English does not become optional. Stretch and Challenge reasoning stays the student's own: the question is now bilingual, the thinking may be in either language. Vocabulary panels, titles and safety lines were already right.

**The skills check is a special case.** The Week 1 and Week 10 papers must be identical for the growth measure to mean anything, so the questions were not changed even though a few stems lack a Chinese gloss. Access comes from the teacher reading the stem and gloss aloud, a reader where recorded in the tracker, and Chinese being accepted for every written answer. The Week 5 checkpoints, not yet sat, were fully paired.

## 3. The policy that came out of the review

`Language_Access_Policy.md` replaces the old rule ("vocabulary bilingual, instructions in English with key words glossed, no whole-paragraph translation") for Years 7 and 8 with a translation matrix: learning goals, instructions, questions, frames, labels, word banks and safety are fully bilingual; Learn content and reading texts get a one-line Chinese key idea or gist; teacher text stays English. English stays first and larger so that capable students are not slowed, and Chinese answers count everywhere. Three student bands (planning only, recorded in tracker column G) decide seating, buddies and starting tier.

Year 7 and Year 8 Weeks 7 to 10, already designed, must be re-generated from the updated paste blocks. The Language support tab in the paste kit holds the new printables and booklets.
- **Native-speaker check.** The volume of Chinese has roughly doubled. Check in the policy's priority order: Today slides and goals, instructions, question stems, word banks and the dictionary, frames, key idea and gist lines, printables. Change the glossary first if a term changes.
- **Printing.** The language support pack (posters, cards, booklets) is for the next lesson, not Week 1. Reprint Weeks 2 to 5 worksheets from the updated files once designed.
- **The skills check** stays as printed. If the Week 1 paper has been sat, Week 10 uses the identical paper.
- **Year 9** is unchanged. The same policy can be applied later with the same scripts; the Year 9 files already follow the same seven-section structure.

## B. Core tasks a Band 1 student could not do, and the fix

These are the places where a student with almost no English was blocked even with the pictures and word bank. Each fix was applied in the file unless marked otherwise.

**Y7 W01**
- Core A: a Band 1 student must read six English sentences to decide O or I; the Chinese instruction and the picture do not help because the task is about the sentences themselves. Fix: pair all six statements (C); optionally print an eye icon beside O and a thought-bubble icon beside I in the heading so the Slide 6 icon system carries to paper, and give one worked example row.
- Core B: producing observations and inferences is doable in Chinese once the frames carry 我观察到___。我推断___，因为___。 and the column headings are paired, but the word bank only covers Stage 3 (fox, bird, wing, feather). Fix: add Stage 1 and 2 nouns (tree 树, seeds 种子, paw print 爪印, stage 阶段) or a labelled picture strip.
- Core B speaker note on Slide 8 tells students to write the table in English, which blocks Band 1 and contradicts J. Fix: change to English or Chinese using the English key words.
- Core A relies on a picture that exists only as a bracketed designer description; if the picture does not print, Band 1 has nothing to look at. Fix: confirm the melted snowman picture is on the worksheet, not just on Slide 6.

**Y7 W02**
- Core A (order the six Galileo captions): the Chinese instruction only says number 1 to 6; the captions themselves are English sentences, so the task is English reading. Fix: a 概要 gist on each caption (the same six gists as the 6b poster) plus the poster's Chinese label, so the student matches caption to panel using the picture; or a picture-ordering version with the six panel thumbnails.
- Core B (cloze): three English explanation sentences; a bilingual word bank tells the student what the words mean but not which gap takes which word. Fix: the Chinese version of each sentence with gaps in the same places, so 实验 maps to experiment, 证据 to evidence; keep the change then measure order so the answer key still holds.
- Core C (tick testable or not): four English questions to judge; without understanding them there is nothing to decide. Fix: full Chinese pair under each question and bilingual column headings; the task is then a Chinese reading and ticking task.
- Question sort (the 18 minute Do task every group does, Slides 8 to 9 and printable 6a): cards are English with one glossed word, and the Lead Scientist reads them aloud in English. Fix: full Chinese question on every card; the language buddy reads the Chinese; bilingual pile headers already exist.
- Exit ticket: Q1 asks the student to choose between two English questions and Q2 produces an English sentence from nothing but a frame. Fix: pair both options and both stems, give the Chinese frame, and state in the answer key that Chinese answers count (it currently says so only for My thinking).
- Do Now mystery cups: workable for Band 1 once the frame is bilingual, because the evidence is physical (shake, listen, weigh) and the inference may be written in Chinese.

**Y7 W03**
- Core A cloze: the student must read six English sentences and pick six English words with no Chinese anywhere. Fix: Chinese frame beneath each sentence with the same gaps (D), word bank EN 中文 (F), and a short cue Copy from the slide 从幻灯片抄写, since Slide 5 completes the same cloze together.
- Core B picture sort: the six pictures carry English-only labels (wing length, fall time, drop height, number of paper clips, type of card, how we let go), so the pictures alone must carry the meaning. Fix: bilingual labels on every picture and 自变量 / 因变量 / 控制变量 inside the boxes; then the task is a true picture sort a Band 1 student can do.
- Core C results table: the Notes column asks two English questions and expects English writing; headings are English only. Fix: bilingual headings and tick boxes for Notes (spun 转了 ☐ / hit something 碰到了东西 ☐) so the record is numbers and ticks only.
- Core D variables table: the student must produce the words wing length, fall time, drop height from nothing under English headings. Fix: fully bilingual headings with the gesture icons, a cue to copy from Core B or the fold-under word bank, and Chinese answers accepted (J).
- Do Now box: question and frame are English only and the picture is on the slide, not the sheet. Fix: pair the question and the frame; the slide picture and say, show, check carry the rest.
- Exit ticket: the Ruby scenario is English only, has no picture on the strip, and the words ball, size and bounce are in no word bank, so a Band 1 student cannot even identify what is changed. Fix: pair the sentence, print the Slide 11 picture as a thumbnail on the strip, and add ball 球 / bounce height 弹起高度 to the fold-under bank.
- Think and Reflect odd one out (needed for the exit ticket thinking): all four cards are English reading. Fix: one 概要 line per card (I) and the Chinese frame on the footer.
