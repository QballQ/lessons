# My Science Dictionary | 我的科学词典: Year 8 Science Detectives

Series: Science Detectives 科学侦探 | A5 booklet, one per student, used every week | Weeks 1 to 10
Chinese text status: draft, needs native-speaker check

---

## 0. How to use (for the teacher)

- Hand the booklet out in Week 1, tucked inside each student's case file 案件档案. Students write their name and class on the cover in the first lesson.
- When a key word comes up in any phase of a lesson, point to its page (routine 6 in `Language_Access_Policy.md`, Section 9). Say the English; a student reads the Chinese.
- In the last minute of Think and Reflect, when there is time, students write their own example on the "My example 我的例子" line for one or two of today's words. A Band 1 student may write the example in Chinese: praise the English key word, accept the Chinese sentence.
- The booklet is open on the desk in every lesson and in the Week 5 checkpoint. It is closed and put away during the skills checks in Weeks 1 and 10, which must run under the same conditions both times.
- It is the student's own book to keep. Do not collect it at the end of the series.
- The fold-under word bank on each worksheet covers the same words for that lesson; the booklet is the long-term version that grows week by week, so a student who loses a worksheet still has every word.
- It contains no case evidence, no statements, no results and nothing about the solution, so it is safe to take home and to show parents.
- Words a student meets outside the lesson go on the back cover ("Words I found by myself"). Look at these now and then; a useful one can be added to the glossary's "Added during the language pass" list.

## 1. Booklet [Paste into Claude Design]

Create an A5 portrait, black-and-white-printable booklet for Year 8, "My Science Dictionary 我的科学词典", using the Think Like a Scientist design system, Science Detectives series. Print spec: A5 portrait (148 mm by 210 mm), saddle-stitched, printed black and white on 90 gsm white paper, 32 pages (8 A4 sheets printed both sides, folded and stapled); if the layout needs more or fewer pages, keep the total a multiple of 4. Copies: one per student plus 4 spares per class. Inside margin at least 12 mm at the spine so the staples do not hide text. Throughout: English first and larger, Chinese beneath or after it in the lighter weight, as in the paired-line style; the Science Detectives series icon (magnifying glass or fingerprint) and its halftone pattern are the only decoration; page numbers at the outer bottom corner of every inside page.

**Cover**: title "My Science Dictionary" with "我的科学词典" beneath it; the Science Detectives series icon and the series name "Science Detectives 科学侦探"; the programme wordmark "Think Like a Scientist 像科学家一样思考" (English above Chinese) at the foot; two fields with writing lines, "Name 姓名: ____________" and "Class 班级: ____________"; and one line in the lighter weight near the bottom: "Think in any language. Science words in English. 思考可用任何语言，科学词汇用英语。"

**Inside cover: How to use this book 怎样使用这本词典** (four short lines for students, each English sentence followed by its Chinese; a small icon from the Instruction words key may sit in front of each line)

1. Find the week, then find the word. 先找到第几周，再找到这个词。
2. Read the English meaning, then the Chinese meaning. 先读英文意思，再读中文意思。
3. Write your own example on the line. Chinese is fine. 在横线上写你自己的例子，可以用中文。
4. Open it in every lesson and in the checkpoint, but not in the skills check. 每节课和阶段检测时都可以打开，技能检测时不能用。

Footer line, lighter weight: "This book is yours to keep. 这本词典是你的，请保存好。"

**One section per week.** Each week starts on a new page with the heading "Week n 第n周 | [English title] / [中文标题]" in the series style, the week number large. Under the heading, one entry for every key word that is new that week. A word already listed in an earlier week is not repeated; instead the week section ends with one line in the lighter weight, "Also this week 本周还会用到: word 中文 (Week n), ...", listing the recurring words with the week where they first appeared.

Entry layout, the same for every word: an entry is a horizontal block across the page, separated from the next by a thin rule. Right of the icon, the English word in bold (the largest text in the entry) with its Chinese directly beneath in the lighter weight. Then the English meaning in the body size with the Chinese meaning beneath it in the lighter weight. Under the whole entry, a sixth element, one writing line the full width of the entry, labelled "My example 我的例子: ______________". Four or five entries fit on a page.
