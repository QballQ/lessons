### 1. Pykrete: the ship made of ice | 木屑冰：用冰造的船

Could you build a ship out of ice? Not a small boat. A giant ship, big enough for aeroplanes to land on.

能用冰造一艘船吗？不是小船，而是大到能让飞机降落的巨轮。

In the Second World War, a British inventor said yes. This is the story of Geoffrey Pyke, and a strange material called pykrete.

第二次世界大战期间，一位英国发明家说：能。这就是杰弗里·派克（Geoffrey Pyke）和“木屑冰”（pykrete）的故事。

### 2. The problem: 1942 | 难题：1942年

The year is 1942. Britain is an island, and it needs food, fuel and weapons. They come across the Atlantic Ocean by ship, from North America.

1942年。英国是岛国，需要粮食、燃料和武器，这些都靠船从北美横渡大西洋运来。

But German submarines, called U-boats, are hunting these ships. Hundreds of ships are sunk.

但是德国潜艇（U型潜艇）在猎杀这些船，数百艘船被击沉。

Aeroplanes can protect the ships, but in 1942 they cannot fly far enough from land. In the middle of the ocean there is a gap with no air cover.

飞机可以保护船队，但1942年的飞机飞不了那么远。大洋中部有一段没有空中掩护的缺口。

The answer is an aircraft carrier: a ship that planes can land on. But carriers are made of steel, and steel is in short supply.

办法是航空母舰：飞机可以在上面起降的船。但航母要用钢来造，而钢材短缺。

### 3. The inventor: Geoffrey Pyke | 发明家：杰弗里·派克

Enter Geoffrey Pyke. He was born in 1893. He was not a trained scientist or engineer. He had been a journalist, and he had started a school.

杰弗里·派克登场。他生于1893年，不是受过专业训练的科学家或工程师。他当过记者，还办过一所学校。

In the First World War he was held prisoner in Germany, and he escaped. He was famous for unusual ideas.

第一次世界大战时，他在德国被关押，后来成功逃脱。他以想法奇特而出名。

In the Second World War he worked for Lord Louis Mountbatten, at a department called Combined Operations. His job was to think of ideas that nobody else would think of.

二战时，他在蒙巴顿勋爵领导的联合作战部工作。他的任务就是想出别人想不到的主意。

### 4. The idea: a ship made of ice | 设想：用冰造船

Pyke looked at icebergs. An iceberg is huge, it floats, and it is very hard to sink. And ice is cheap: it is only frozen water.

派克想到了冰山。冰山巨大、能漂浮、很难击沉。而且冰很便宜：它只是冻住的水。

So why not build an aircraft carrier out of ice? A floating airfield, in the middle of the Atlantic.

那为什么不用冰造一艘航空母舰？一座漂在大西洋中部的浮动机场。

The plan was given a secret code name: Project Habakkuk.

这个计划有一个秘密代号：哈巴谷计划（Project Habakkuk）。

### 5. Two problems with ice | 冰的两个问题

But there are two big problems with ice. First, ice is brittle. Hit it, and it cracks and shatters.

但冰有两个大问题。第一，冰很脆（brittle）：一撞就裂、就碎。

Second, ice melts. A ship that melts is not a very good ship.

第二，冰会熔化。会化掉的船可不是好船。

### 6. The fix: ice plus wood | 解决办法：冰加木屑

The answer came from the laboratory. In New York, two scientists, Herman Mark and Walter Hohenstein, added wood fibres to water before freezing it. The ice became much stronger.

答案来自实验室。在纽约，两位科学家马克和霍恩施泰因在水结冰前加入木纤维，冰变得结实多了。

In London, a young scientist called Max Perutz tested the new material. His secret laboratory was a meat freezer, under Smithfield Market. He later won a Nobel Prize for other work.

在伦敦，年轻科学家马克斯·佩鲁茨（Max Perutz）测试了这种新材料。他的秘密实验室是史密斯菲尔德市场地下的一间冷冻肉库。他后来因其他研究获得诺贝尔奖。

The best recipe was about 14 percent sawdust and 86 percent ice, by mass. They named it pykrete: Pyke, plus concrete.

最佳配方按质量计约为14%木屑、86%冰。它被命名为pykrete：Pyke（派克）加concrete（混凝土）。

### 7. Why is pykrete stronger? | 木屑冰为什么更结实？

Why is pykrete stronger? In plain ice, a small crack grows quickly, right through the block. In pykrete, the wood fibres hold the ice together and stop the cracks from growing.

木屑冰为什么更结实？普通冰里，小裂缝会很快贯穿整块冰。木屑冰里，木纤维把冰拉在一起，阻止裂缝扩大。

It is a composite material: two materials working together, like straw in mud bricks, or steel bars in concrete.

它是一种复合材料（composite）：两种材料共同起作用，就像泥砖里的稻草、混凝土里的钢筋。

Pykrete also melts more slowly. When the outside melts, it leaves a layer of wet sawdust. That layer is a thermal insulator. It slows the heat getting in, just like the materials you wrapped around your ice cubes.

木屑冰也熔化得更慢。表面熔化后留下一层湿木屑，这层木屑是隔热材料，能减慢热量进入，就像你们包在冰块外面的材料一样。

### 8. The demonstration: Quebec, 1943 | 演示：魁北克，1943年

In August 1943, the leaders of Britain, the United States and Canada met in Quebec. Mountbatten brought two blocks: one of plain ice, and one of pykrete.

1943年8月，英国、美国和加拿大的领导人在魁北克开会。蒙巴顿带来两块东西：一块普通冰，一块木屑冰。

The story goes that he took out his gun and fired at the ice. It shattered. Then he fired at the pykrete. The bullet bounced off, and flew across the room.

据说他拔出手枪向冰块开枪，冰碎了。接着他向木屑冰开枪，子弹弹开，飞过了房间。

People who were there told the story in different ways, so historians say: reportedly. That is good science too. Be honest about how sure you are.

在场的人说法不一，所以历史学家会说“据说”。这也是好的科学态度：如实说明自己有多大把握。

### 9. The plan: a giant | 计划：一个庞然大物

The planned ship was enormous. About 600 metres long and 90 metres wide. That is longer than five football pitches.

计划中的船极其巨大：长约600米，宽约90米，比五个足球场连起来还长。

Its walls would be 12 metres thick, and it would weigh about two million tonnes. No ship that big has ever been built.

船壁厚12米，重约200万吨。至今从未有人造出过这么大的船。

Cold pipes inside the hull would keep it frozen. The designers hoped that holes could be repaired with more pykrete.

船体内的制冷管道让它保持冰冻。设计者希望破洞可以用更多的木屑冰修补。

### 10. The model: Patricia Lake, Canada | 模型：加拿大帕特里夏湖

To test the idea, a small model was built in 1943, on Patricia Lake in Canada.

为了检验这个设想，1943年人们在加拿大的帕特里夏湖上造了一个小模型。

It was 18 metres long and 9 metres wide, and weighed about a thousand tonnes. A small refrigeration unit kept it frozen.

它长18米、宽9米，重约1000吨。一台小型制冷机让它保持冰冻。

It worked. The model stayed frozen through the summer of 1943.

成功了。模型在1943年整个夏天都没有化掉。

### 11. Why was it never built? | 为什么最终没有建造？

So why was the giant ship never built? First, cost. It needed far more money, workers and time than expected. Even the cold pipes needed huge amounts of steel.

那巨轮为什么没有造出来？第一是成本：需要的钱、人力和时间远超预期，连制冷管道也要用大量的钢。

Second, the problem went away. New aeroplanes could fly further, and new air bases closed the gap in the Atlantic.

第二，难题自己消失了：新飞机飞得更远，新的空军基地补上了大西洋的缺口。

The project was stopped. The model was left to melt, and what remains of it still lies at the bottom of Patricia Lake today.

计划终止了。模型被留在原地慢慢融化，它的残骸至今仍躺在帕特里夏湖底。

### 12. Was it a failure? | 这算失败吗？

Was it a failure? Not really. The team asked a bold question, tested it with careful experiments, and built a model before the real thing.

这算失败吗？其实不算。团队提出了大胆的问题，用认真的实验去检验，并且先造模型再造实物。

And when the evidence said stop, they stopped. That is how science and engineering work.

当证据表明该停下时，他们就停下了。科学和工程就是这样运作的。

### 13. Your turn, Ice Engineers | 轮到你们了，冰雪工程师

Now it is your turn. You are the ice engineers.

现在轮到你们了。你们就是冰雪工程师。

You could ask: does more sawdust make ice stronger? Which fibre works best: sawdust, tissue, shredded paper or cotton wool? Does pykrete really melt more slowly?

你们可以问：木屑越多，冰越结实吗？哪种纤维最好：木屑、纸巾、碎纸还是棉花？木屑冰真的熔化得更慢吗？

Remember the fair test. Change one thing. Measure one thing. Keep everything else the same. And repeat your test.

记住公平测试：只改变一个量，只测量一个量，其他都保持不变，并且重复测试。

Geoffrey Pyke never built his ship. But perhaps you can show that his idea was right.

杰弗里·派克没能造出他的船。但也许你们能证明他的想法是对的。
