What is pykrete?什么是木屑冰？

Pykrete = ice + wood fibres (sawdust). The best recipe was about 14% sawdust and 86% ice, by mass.

木屑冰 = 冰 + 木纤维（木屑）。最佳配方按质量计约为14%木屑、86%冰。

It is stronger than plain ice and it melts more slowly.

它比普通冰更结实，熔化得也更慢。

The story in 30 seconds30秒故事

In 1942, in the Second World War, steel was in short supply. The inventor Geoffrey Pyke wanted to build a giant aircraft carrier out of ice. Plain ice is brittle and it melts, so scientists added sawdust. A model was built on a lake in Canada in 1943 and it stayed frozen all summer. The giant ship was never built: it cost too much, and it was no longer needed.

1942年二战期间钢材短缺，发明家杰弗里·派克想用冰造一艘巨型航空母舰。普通冰又脆又会熔化，于是科学家加入了木屑。1943年在加拿大的湖上造了一个模型，整个夏天都没有化。巨轮最终没有建造：成本太高，而且已经不再需要。

Why does it work? Three ideas为什么有效？三个要点

1Fibres stop cracks 纤维挡住裂缝

In plain ice, a small crack grows quickly, right through. In pykrete, the fibres hold the ice together and the crack stops.

普通冰里，小裂缝很快贯穿。木屑冰里，纤维把冰拉在一起，裂缝被挡住。

2A composite material 复合材料

A composite is two materials working together. Each one does a job the other cannot do. Ice is hard but brittle; fibres are bendy but tough.

复合材料是两种材料共同起作用，各自完成对方做不到的事。冰硬但脆；纤维软但韧。

3Sawdust insulates 木屑能隔热

When the outside melts, a layer of wet sawdust is left. It slows the heat getting in, like the materials around your ice cubes last week.

表面熔化后留下一层湿木屑，它减慢热量进入，就像上周包在冰块外面的材料。

Did you know?你知道吗？

Pyke used 14% sawdust. Our strips use much less (up to 0.9 g in 12 ml of water, about 7%), so more sawdust should still help. But too much sawdust means not enough ice to hold the fibres.

派克用了14%的木屑。我们的冰条用得少得多（12毫升水里最多0.9克，约7%），所以多加木屑应该仍有帮助。但木屑太多，冰就不够把纤维粘住。
