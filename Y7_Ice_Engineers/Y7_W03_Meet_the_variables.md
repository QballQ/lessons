## 1. Lesson plan (one page, for the teacher)

- **Big idea:** In a fair test we change only one thing, measure one thing, and keep everything else the same.
- **Learning goals:**
  - I can name the independent, dependent and control variables in an experiment.
  - I can explain why a fair test changes only one thing.
- **Key words:**

| English | 中文 | Student-friendly meaning |
|---|---|---|
| variable | 变量 | anything that can change in an experiment |
| independent variable | 自变量 | the one thing I change |
| dependent variable | 因变量 | the thing I measure |
| control variable | 控制变量 | a thing I keep the same |
| fair test | 公平测试 | a test where only one thing changes |
| air resistance | 空气阻力 | the push of air against something moving through it |
| spinner | 纸直升机 | a paper toy with two wings that spins as it falls |
| wing length | 翼长 | how long each wing of the spinner is, in cm |

- **Timeline:**

| Phase | Min | Teacher does | Students do | Resource |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Do Now | 4 | Shows a picture of two paper planes thrown by different people from different heights | "Is this a fair race?" Answer with the frame: "It is / is not fair because ___." | Slide 1; worksheet Do Now box |
| Hook | 3 | Drops a short-winged (4 cm) and a long-winged (8 cm) spinner together from the 2 m mark | Vote on which lands first, then watch | Slide 2 |
| Learn | 7 | Teaches "I change / I measure / I keep the same" with hand gestures; bilingual vocabulary panel; safety slide | Copy the gestures; complete the cloze on the slide together | Slides 3 to 6; worksheet Core A |
| Do | 18 | Circulates; checks roles, drop height and timing | Groups cut and fold three pre-printed spinners (4, 6 and 8 cm wings), drop each from the 2 m mark, time and record; complete the variables table | Slides 7 to 8; spinner template; worksheet Core B, results table, Stretch, Challenge |
| Think and Reflect | 8 | Shows four test descriptions; collects exit tickets | Odd one out: which test is not fair? Pair talk in any language, share in English with a frame. Exit ticket: name the IV, DV and one control variable | Slides 9 to 11; odd-one-out cards; exit ticket |

- **Tiers:**
  - Core: cloze with word bank, and a picture sort into I change / I measure / I keep the same; fill in the results table.
  - Stretch: name two more control variables and explain why each must stay the same.
  - Challenge: design a new test with paper type as the independent variable and predict the result using "air resistance".
- **Language support:**
  - Frames: "It is / is not fair because ___." "I change ___. I measure ___. I keep ___ the same." "We must keep ___ the same because ___." "Test ___ is the odd one out because ___."
  - Chinese talk welcome in the Do Now and the odd-one-out discussion.
  - Vocabulary traps: "independent" and "dependent" look alike: link them to the gestures (I change = independent, it is my choice; I measure = dependent, it depends on what I changed). "Control" here means "keep the same" (控制), not "control a person". "Fair" means equal and honest, not "light-coloured".
- **Misconceptions to watch for:**
  - "Heavier things always fall faster." The spinners all carry one paper clip; what changes the fall time is wing length and air resistance.
  - "Changing two things at once gets results faster." Then we cannot tell which change caused the result.
  - "The dependent variable is the thing that stays the same." No: it is what we measure.
- **Safety:**
  - No standing on chairs or tables. The Dropper stands flat on the floor.
  - Care with scissors: carry with points down; cut away from fingers.
  - Clear the floor below the 2 m mark of bags.
- **If time is short:** test two wing lengths (4 cm and 8 cm) instead of three.
- **Assessment and handover links:**
  - Variables table and first results on the worksheet.
  - Exit ticket: IV, DV and one control variable (key skill for the Week 5 checkpoint).
  - Spinners stored in a labelled group envelope for Week 4.

## 4. Answer key (for the teacher)

**Do Now:** It is not fair because they throw from different heights (also: different people throw; the planes are different shapes; they may throw with different force).

**Core A:** length, independent, time, dependent, same, control.

**Core B:** (1) wing length: I change. (2) fall time: I measure. (3) drop height, (4) number of paper clips, (5) type of card, (6) how we let go: I keep the same.

**Core C (results):** class data will vary. Expect roughly 1.2 to 1.5 s for 4 cm wings, 1.5 to 1.9 s for 6 cm and 1.8 to 2.3 s for 8 cm from 2 m. The pattern to look for: longer wings, longer fall time. Do not worry if a group's times do not follow the pattern; Week 4 deals with repeats and anomalies.

**Core D:** I change: wing length. I measure: fall time. I keep the same (any three): drop height; number of paper clips (one); type of card; the same person timing; the same way of letting go; same room, no fans or open windows; same body size of spinner.

**Stretch (Secure examples):**
- "We must keep the drop height the same because a spinner dropped from higher up takes longer to fall, so the test would not be fair."
- "We must keep the number of paper clips the same because more paper clips make the spinner heavier, so it would fall faster and we could not tell if it was the wings."
- "We must keep the same person timing because people press the stopwatch at different speeds."

**Challenge (Secure example):**
- IV: type of paper (e.g. thin printer paper, card, tissue paper). DV: fall time (s). Controls: wing length, drop height, number of paper clips, size and shape of the spinner.
- Prediction: "I predict the card spinner will fall fastest because it is heavier, but it has the same wing size, so the air resistance is about the same. The pull of gravity is bigger compared with the air resistance, so it speeds up more. The thin paper spinner will fall more slowly because air resistance has a bigger effect on a lighter spinner of the same shape." Also accept: "Tissue paper wings might bend and not spin, so it might fall fast and messily." Excellent: notes that changing the paper also changes mass and stiffness, so the test needs care.
- Note for the teacher: this is not the same as "heavier objects fall faster" in general. With identical shapes, the lighter spinner is affected more by the same air resistance. Without air, all would fall together.

**Odd one out (Section 6b):** B is not fair because the two spinners are dropped from different heights (2 m and 1 m). Also credit: D, because different people time each spinner, so reaction times differ (this is a good point and leads into Week 4). A and C are fair.

**My thinking (guidance):** If you change more than one thing, you do not know which change caused the result. Accept Chinese.

**Exit ticket:** IV: size of the ball. DV: bounce height (cm). Control (any one): drop height; type of ball or material; the floor surface; how it is dropped (not thrown); the person measuring.

- Never stand on chairs or tables to drop spinners. Stand flat on the floor.

**Each template (measurements from the top edge of the rectangle, in cm):**
- Outer edge: solid cut line all round, 5 cm x 19 cm.
- **Wings (0 to 8 cm from the top):** a solid centre cut line runs straight down the middle from the top edge (at 2.5 cm from each side) to 8 cm from the top. This makes two wings, each 2.5 cm wide. Label the left wing "A: fold forward 向前折" with a small curved arrow, and the right wing "B: fold back 向后折" with a curved arrow in the other direction.
- **Wing fold line (8 cm from the top):** a horizontal dashed line across the full 5 cm width at 8 cm from the top, where the centre cut ends. The wings fold down along this line in opposite directions.
- **Wing-length marks (trim lines):** these set the three wing lengths, measured from the wing fold line up to the top of the wing.
  - L (long, 8 cm wings): no trimming. Print "L = 8 cm" in small text near the top edge.
  - Dotted line across both wings at 2 cm from the top, labelled at its right end "M: cut here for 6 cm wings | 剪到这里：6厘米".
  - Dotted line across both wings at 4 cm from the top, labelled at its right end "S: cut here for 4 cm wings | 剪到这里：4厘米".
  - A small ruler scale (0 to 8 cm) printed along the left edge of wing A, with 0 at the wing fold line, so students can check the length.
- **Hub (8 to 9 cm from the top):** full width, no lines inside.
- **Side cuts (9 cm from the top):** two short solid horizontal cut lines, each 1.5 cm long, going in from the left edge and from the right edge at 9 cm from the top. This leaves a 2 cm wide uncut gap in the middle.
- **Side fold lines (9 cm to 19 cm from the top):** two vertical dashed lines, 1.5 cm in from each side edge, from the end of each side cut down to the bottom edge. Label the strips between the fold lines and the edges "fold in 向内折". When both side flaps are folded in, the stem is 2 cm wide.
- **Bottom fold line (17 cm from the top):** a horizontal dashed line across the stem at 17 cm from the top. Label: "fold up 向上折".
- **Paper clip position:** a small grey outline of a paper clip drawn on the stem between 16 cm and 19 cm from the top, centred, with the label "paper clip here 回形针夹在这里". After folding the bottom 2 cm up, push one paper clip fully onto the folded end, in the centre.
- **Name label:** a small box on the stem (between 10 cm and 14 cm from the top) reading "Group ____ Wing length ____ cm".

1. Cut round the outside solid line. 沿外框实线剪下。
2. Cut down the centre line to make two wings. 沿中间实线剪开，形成两个翼。
