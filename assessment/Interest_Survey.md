# Interest Survey | 兴趣问卷

All series (Years 7, 8 and 9) | Week 1 and Week 10 | 3 minutes
Chinese text status: draft, needs native-speaker check

---

## 0. How to run the survey (for the teacher)

- **Same survey twice:** Week 1 (straight after the skills check) and Week 10 (before certificates). Use fresh copies each time.
- **Timing:** 3 minutes. Read each statement aloud in English once; students read the Chinese themselves.
- **Say before they start:** "There are no right or wrong answers. This does not change your award. Please be honest." 没有对错之分，不影响你的奖项，请如实填写。
- **Names are needed** so Week 1 and Week 10 can be compared in the tracker. Results are only shared as class averages (programme summary for principals), never by name.
- **Scoring:** each face scores 1 to 5 (left to right). Enter each statement's score in the tracker (W1 S1 to S6, W10 S1 to S6). The tracker works out each student's mean and the change.
- **Open question:** optional. Do not score it. Type any useful ideas into the tracker notes or the programme summary (anonymised).

## 1. Survey sheet [Paste into Claude Design]

Create an A4 portrait, black-and-white-printable survey (half A4 per copy, two copies per sheet, with a cut line across the middle) for the Think Like a Scientist interest survey, using the Think Like a Scientist design system. No series colour: use the programme wordmark only, so one version serves Years 7, 8 and 9. Minimum 12 pt text. Each statement is one row: English statement, Chinese beneath in a lighter weight, then five circles with simple line-drawn faces to tick. Faces must read clearly in greyscale (draw the mouth shape, do not rely on colour).

**Header**
- Title: How do you feel about science? / 你对科学有什么感受？

- Tick box: Week 1 ☐  Week 10 ☐
- Instruction: Tick (✓) ONE face for each sentence. There are no right or wrong answers. 每句话只勾选一个表情。没有对错之分。

## 2. Scoring guide (for the teacher)

| Face (left to right) | Score |
|---|---|
| Strongly disagree 非常不同意 | 1 |
| Disagree 不同意 | 2 |
| Not sure 不确定 | 3 |
| Agree 同意 | 4 |
| Strongly agree 非常同意 | 5 |

- If a student ticks two faces for one statement, enter the lower score. If a statement is blank, leave the cell blank (the tracker mean ignores blanks).
- **What to report:** for each class, the mean score for each statement in Week 1 and Week 10, and the change. A change of +0.3 or more on a statement is worth mentioning in the programme summary; a change of less than 0.3 is likely to be noise in a class of 24.
- Statement 4 (confidence with science English) and Statement 5 (checking evidence) are the two most closely linked to the programme aims: report these first.
