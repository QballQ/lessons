## 0. How to run the checkpoint (for the teacher)

- **When:** Week 5, after the mystery liquid test (see the Week 5 lesson plan: indicator ready in cups, checkpoint 10 minutes). 10 minutes working, plus 1 minute to give out and collect.
- **What it covers:** question types 1 to 6 of the skills check, with new questions set in the contexts of Weeks 1 to 4 (scene observations, fingerprints, ink chromatography, the white powder tests).
- **Language:** read any question aloud in English if asked. Explain everyday words, not science words. Accept Chinese for the explanation in Question 4. Under the language access policy (Section 7.2) this now applies to every written answer on the paper, not only Question 4: 3a to 3c, 4a, 4b and both Question 5 headings may be written in Chinese and earn the mark on the same rules as English. Every stem, scenario sentence, tick-box option, table heading and row label on the paper carries a Chinese line beneath it. When a student asks for a question to be read, read the English stem and then the Chinese line beneath it, or ask a student to read the Chinese line aloud. A reader may read both the English and the Chinese aloud to a student who needs it; record any reader or other access arrangement in the tracker notes column, as for the skills check.
- **Give-out minute (say, show, check):** as the papers go out, point to the Chinese line under the first stem and say, in English and Chinese, that every question has one and that any answer may be written in Chinese. Thumbs check, then start the 10 minutes.
- **Marking Chinese answers:** mark the science, not the language. Chinese answers that earn the mark include: 3a 溶剂 or 溶剂的种类 or 水、盐水和醋; 3b 墨水移动的距离 or 墨水移动了多远 or 墨水在5分钟内移动的距离; 3c 同样的墨水, 同样的滤纸条, 同样的时间（5分钟）, 同样多的溶剂 or 同样大的墨点; 4a 滴数不同 or 醋的量不一样 (2滴和10滴); 4b 两种粉末加同样多的醋 or 滴数相同; 5 column 1 溶剂 or 液体 (no unit), column 2 墨水移动距离（厘米） or 距离（厘米）; 6a 2.1; 6b 4.5. A marker who reads no Chinese passes those papers to a Chinese-speaking colleague or uses a translation app, and notes this in the tracker notes column. The mark scheme in Section 2 stays in English.
- **Unlike the skills check, you can go through the answers** in Week 6. The checkpoint is not reused.
- **Record** the score out of 12 in the tracker (column "W5 checkpoint") before the handover note is written. The incoming teacher observes this lesson.
