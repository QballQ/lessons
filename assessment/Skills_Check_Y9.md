# Y9 Skills Check | 科学技能检测

Series: Claim Busters 真相调查员 | Week 1 (baseline) and Week 10 (final) | 15 minutes | 20 marks
Chinese text status: draft, needs native-speaker check

---

## 0. How to run the skills check (for the teacher)

- **Same paper twice.** This exact paper is used in Week 1 (baseline) and Week 10 (final). Growth = Week 10 score minus Week 1 score.
- **Do not go through the answers after Week 1.** Collect every paper, mark it, enter the scores in the tracker and keep the papers in the class assessment folder. Do not hand them back or display answers until after Week 10, or the growth measure is lost.
- **Timing:** 1 minute to give out papers and write names, 15 minutes working, papers collected at once. Give a 5-minute warning.
- **Language:** you may read any question aloud in English, once or twice. You may explain everyday words (for example "torch", "scarf", "lid"). Do not explain science words (for example "variable", "anomaly", "mean", "range", "claim"), because knowing them is part of what the paper measures.
- **Answers in Chinese** are accepted for explanations (Questions 4, 8, 9 and 10). Spelling and grammar are not marked if the science is clear.
- **No calculators needed.** Students may write working on the paper.
- **Support:** students who need it may have a reader. Record any access arrangement in the tracker notes column.
- **What the paper does not need:** none of the claims on this paper are in the Week 7 claim bank, and no knowledge from the series is needed. Every question gives the information needed.

## 2. Mark scheme (for the teacher) [Paste into Claude Design]

Create an A4 portrait, black-and-white-printable mark scheme for the Year 9 Skills Check, using the Think Like a Scientist design system, Claim Busters series. One table per question: Question | Answer | Accept | Do not accept | Marks. Teacher-only document: English only is fine.

**General rules**
- Mark the science, not the English. Accept Chinese or mixed answers. Ignore spelling if the meaning is clear.
- No half marks.
- If a student gives two answers and one is wrong, do not give the mark (unless the question asks for more than one).

| Q | Answer | Accept | Do not accept | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A: O. B: I. C: O. D: I. | | | 4 correct = 2. 3 correct = 1. 2 or fewer = 0. |
| 2 | B | | More than one box ticked | 1 |
| 3a | Whether the cup has a lid or not | "the lid"; "lid / no lid" | "the cup"; "the tea" | 1 |
| 3b | The temperature of the tea (after 20 minutes) | "how hot the tea is"; "temperature" | "time" (time is fixed at 20 minutes); "heat" alone | 1 |
| 3c | Any one: volume of tea (200 ml); type of cup; starting temperature; time (20 minutes); same room | "same amount of tea"; "same cups" | "the lid" (that is the IV) | 1 |
| 4a | Any one: the torches are different (small and big, bright); one battery is new and one is old | "different torches"; "the big torch uses more power"; "old battery" | "different brands" (that is what is being tested) | 1 |

**What separates the levels (use when reviewing scripts)**
- Questions 1 to 3, 5 and 6a are accessible to most students: a Beginning student often scores here only.
- Questions 6b and 6c (excluding the anomaly), 7 (reading two lines and subtracting), 8 (second mark: values from the graph), 9 (a matching reason) and 10 (naming the third factor) separate Secure and Excellent students.
