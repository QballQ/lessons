## 0. How to run the skills check (for the teacher)

- **Same paper twice.** This exact paper is used in Week 1 (baseline) and Week 10 (final). Growth = Week 10 score minus Week 1 score. The language access policy (00_Language_Support/Language_Access_Policy.md, Section 7.1) does not change this: the student paper in Section 1 is left exactly as it was, including every Chinese gloss line, and the identical paper must be used in Week 10. If Week 1 has already been sat, do not reprint the paper, even to correct a gloss. Access is given orally and through marking, as the bullets below say, and whatever access is given in Week 1 is given in the same way in Week 10.
- **Do not go through the answers after Week 1.** Collect every paper, mark it, enter the scores in the tracker and keep the papers in the class assessment folder. Do not hand them back or display answers until after Week 10, or the growth measure is lost.
- **Timing:** 1 minute to give out papers and write names, 15 minutes working, papers collected at once. Give a 5-minute warning.
- **Language:** you may read any question aloud in English, once or twice. You may explain everyday words (for example "trophy", "drawer"). Do not explain science words (for example "variable", "anomaly", "mean", "evidence"), because knowing them is part of what the paper measures. Under the language access policy you also read the printed Chinese gloss aloud after the English: the printed gloss is part of the paper, so reading it is not explaining a science word. Read the English stem, then its Chinese gloss, once or twice, in exactly the same way in Week 1 and Week 10. The Instruction words key poster and the Classroom language card may stay on the wall: Tick, Write, Work out, Rank and Check are instruction words, not the science the paper measures.
- **Answers in Chinese** are accepted for explanations (Questions 4, 8, 9 and 10). Spelling and grammar are not marked if the science is clear. Under the language access policy Chinese is accepted for every written answer on the paper, not only these four: this includes Questions 3a to 3c, the Question 5 headings (a Chinese heading with the correct unit symbol earns the mark), the Question 6b working and Question 10b. The mark scheme general rule "mark the science, not the English" already says this. Tell the class before they start that any written answer may be in Chinese, because the printed header line says only "some answers". Apply the same rule in both weeks.
- **No calculators needed.** Students may write working on the paper.
- **Support:** students who need it may have a reader. Record any access arrangement in the tracker notes column. The reader may read the English stem and the Chinese gloss aloud, once or twice (the printed gloss, or the agreed oral gloss listed in the next bullet where the paper has none), and may not explain, hint or help with the answer. Record the reader, and whether the oral gloss was used, in both weeks.
- **Oral Chinese gloss for items with no printed gloss.** The paper is not changed this term, so these items are read in English and given a short oral Chinese gloss of their meaning by the teacher or the reader, in both weeks: Question 1 sentences A to D and the table headings (Sentence; O or I?); Question 2 options A to C; the Question 3 scenario (five sentences) and sub-stems a to c; Question 4 panel labels (Brand A, Brand B, 10 seconds, 1 minute); Question 6 table headings (Try; Time to dissolve) and sub-stem a; Question 7 graph title and axis labels (point to each axis and say which is time and which is temperature); Question 10 table headings (Clue; What the team found) and clues X, Y and Z. Agree the wording with a Chinese-speaking colleague before Week 1 and keep a written copy in the class assessment folder so that Week 10 uses the same wording. Give the meaning of the words on the paper only; do not add hints or explain the science.
- **The case on this paper ("The Missing Trophy") is not the Frozen Notebook case**, so the paper gives nothing away and needs no knowledge from the series.
- **For the next cohort (do not change this term):** the language audit found these glossary conflicts and missing glosses in the student paper. Correct them in a new version of the paper before a future Week 1, after the native-speaker check, never between Week 1 and Week 10 of one cohort.
  - Glossary conflicts: the file title and paper title render Skills Check as 科学技能检测 (glossary: 技能检测); "Detective" before a name is 探员 (林探员, 胡探员, 博探员) while the glossary key word detective is 侦探, and the same paper uses 侦探小组 for detective team; Question 10a glosses strongest / weakest as 最强 / 最弱 (glossary strong / weak evidence: 有力 / 薄弱, so 最有力 / 最薄弱); the Question 8 frame Chinese 随着______增加，______会______。 differs from the glossary frame 随着___增加，___增加／减少。 and writes its gaps as ______ not ___; the names Lin, Hu and Bo are transliterated 林, 胡, 博 although policy rule L keeps names in letters (decide, and record the decision in glossary Part 5).
  - Missing glosses: header tick box Week 1 ☐ Week 10 ☐ (第1周 ☐ 第10周 ☐); Total, Year 8 and marks (总分, 八年级, 分); the header line "You can write some answers in Chinese" (should say every written answer); Question 1 table headings Sentence and O or I? (句子; 观察（O）还是推断（I）？) and sentences A to D; Question 2 options A to C; the Question 3 scenario sentences and sub-stems a to c (full Chinese stems, not only the bracketed key word); Question 4 panel labels Brand A and Brand B (A品牌 / B品牌; the clocks may read 10 s / 1 min); Question 6 table headings Try and Time to dissolve (s) (次数; 溶解时间（秒）), sub-stem a (哪个时间是异常值？) and the labels Working: and Mean = (过程： / 平均值 =); Question 7 graph title and axis labels (茶的温度随时间变化; 泡茶后的时间（分钟）; 温度（°C）) and the answer line minutes ago (___分钟前); Question 10 table headings Clue and What the team found (线索; 小组的发现) and one gist line 概要： for each of clues X, Y and Z. Picture and graph labels are set inside the images, so write the bilingual labels into the design brief as well as the text.

## 2. Mark scheme (for the teacher) [Paste into Claude Design]

Create an A4 portrait, black-and-white-printable mark scheme for the Year 8 Skills Check, using the Think Like a Scientist design system, Science Detectives series. One table per question: Question | Answer | Accept | Do not accept | Marks. Teacher-only document: English only is fine.

**General rules**
- Mark the science, not the English. Accept Chinese or mixed answers. Ignore spelling if the meaning is clear.
- No half marks.
- If a student gives two answers and one is wrong, do not give the mark (unless the question asks for more than one).

| Q | Answer | Accept | Do not accept | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A: O. B: I. C: O. D: I. | | | 4 correct = 2. 3 correct = 1. 2 or fewer = 0. |
| 2 | B | | More than one box ticked | 1 |
| 3a | The height the fake blood is dropped from | "height"; "20, 40, 60, 80 cm"; "how high" | "the blood"; "the paper" | 1 |
| 3b | The width of the stain | "size of the stain"; "how big the stain is"; "diameter" | "the stain" alone; "height" | 1 |
| 3c | Any one: same dropper (same size of drop); same type of paper; same liquid (fake blood) | "same amount of blood"; "same room" | "height" (that is the IV); "the stain" | 1 |
| 4a | Any one: the towels are dipped for different times (10 seconds and 1 minute); the pieces of towel are different sizes | "one is big, one is small"; "different time in the water" | "different brands" (that is what is being tested) | 1 |
| 4b | A fix that matches 4a: dip both for the same time; use pieces of the same size | A fix for either problem, even if 4a is blank or wrong | "do it again" with no change; "use the same brand" | 1 |
| 5 | Column 1: Drop height (cm). Column 2: Splash width (mm). | "height (cm)"; "height dropped from (cm)"; "width of splash (mm)"; "splash size (mm)" | A heading with no unit; columns swapped; "width (cm)" (the values are millimetres); "size" with no unit | 1 mark per column: heading and correct unit both needed |
| 6a | 58 s | "58"; "try 4" | | 1 |
| 6b | (32 + 35 + 33 + 30) ÷ 4 = 130 ÷ 4 = 32.5 s | "32.5" with no unit | "33" or "32" (rounded) unless working shows 130 ÷ 4 (then 1 mark) | 2 marks for 32.5. 1 mark for 37.6 (mean of all five: 188 ÷ 5) or for correct method with one arithmetic slip |
| 7 | 20 minutes | 19 to 21 | | 1 |
| 8 | As the time increases, the temperature of the tea decreases. | "falls"; "goes down"; "the tea gets colder" | "the tea gets hotter" | 1 mark: correct trend naming both variables. 1 mark: uses data, for example "from 80 °C at 0 minutes to 27 °C at 50 minutes", or says it falls quickly at first and then more slowly (20 °C in the first 10 minutes, only 4 °C in the last 10 minutes). |
| 9 | Improvement plus a matching reason. Examples: use a magnifying glass or microscope, because you can see small details (colour, thickness, shape); compare the hair with hairs from all the suspects (reference samples), because more than one person may have similar hair; ask another person to compare without knowing which hair is whose, because he may be biased; compare more than one hair, because one hair may not be typical | "look more carefully" only if linked to a tool or a reason about detail | "be more careful" with no detail; a reason that does not match the improvement | 1 improvement, 1 reason |
| 10a | Y, Z, X | | Any other order | 1 |
| 10b | Clue Y matches only one suspect (Suspect 3) / fingerprints are unique to one person | "it points to one person"; "the others match many people" | "fingerprints are important" with no reason; any reason when 10a does not rank Y first | 1 |

**Total: 20**

**What separates the levels (use when reviewing scripts)**
- Questions 1 to 3, 5 and 7 are accessible to most students: a Beginning student often scores here only.
- Questions 6b (excluding the anomaly), 7 (reading the graph backwards from temperature to time), 8 (second mark: describing the shape or quoting data), 9 (a matching reason) and 10 (judging evidence by how many suspects it fits) separate Secure and Excellent students.

**Indicative bands (for the skills report, not for awards)**

| Score | 0 to 5 | 6 to 10 | 11 to 15 | 16 to 20 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Band | Beginning 起步 | Developing 发展 | Secure 达标 | Excellent 优秀 |

Enter the total in the tracker (column "W1 skills check" or "W10 skills check"). Optional: enter question-by-question marks on the "Question analysis" tab to see which skills need reteaching.
