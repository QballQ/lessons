# Year 9 Claim Busters: full resource package

Think Like a Scientist, Year 9 series, Weeks 1 to 10. This is the original Year 9 version: English first, with Chinese for titles, key words and main ideas. It has not had the language access pass that Years 7 and 8 had.

## What is in the folder

| Folder or file | What it is |
|---|---|
| `00_Read_me` | This page |
| `01_Teacher_documents` | Teacher Guide, Programme Outline, the Year 9 resources and prep workbook, all ten one-pagers in one PDF, and the tracker |
| `02_Assessment` | Year 9 Skills Check (Weeks 1 and 10), Year 9 Checkpoint (Week 5), the designed rubric, certificates and reports, and the rubric teacher notes |
| `Year_9_Claim_Busters/Week_NN_Title` | For each week: slides as editable PowerPoint and as PDF, the printables as PDF (worksheet, cards, kit card and other sheets), the full lesson file as PDF, and a one-page summary for the teacher |

## How to use it

1. Read the week's one-pager first: lesson flow, prep, printing, kit and safety on one side of A4.
2. Teach from the PowerPoint (speaker notes are in the notes pane) or the slides PDF.
3. Print from the printables PDF at Actual size (100%), not "Fit to page".
4. The workbook `Resources_and_prep_Weeks_1_to_10_Y9.xlsx` lists every resource for every lesson, the prep time by week, and the open questions to settle before each lesson. Type your number of Year 9 classes in the yellow cell.

## Things to know

- The PowerPoint files use Garamond for titles (it ships with Microsoft Office) in place of the design's EB Garamond, so titles fit their boxes. Text is editable; diagrams are pictures.
- Week 6 (The Mpemba mystery) has a Plan A (ice) and a Plan B (no ice): run one only. In the workbook, items and tasks for one plan start with "Plan A" or "Plan B", and the totals count both.
- Week 8 (Investigation day) kit depends on which claims the groups chose in Week 7: collect the approved plans and check the kit against them.
- The prep minutes in the workbook and one-pagers are mostly estimates, because the Year 9 kit cards give one total per lesson.
- Year 9 items have not been compared line by line with the science requisition workbook.
- Chinese text status: draft, needs native-speaker check.
