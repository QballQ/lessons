Think Like a Scientist brand board

The programme sub-brand, its three series, and the templates every lesson uses. Classroom materials carry only the programme brand. The HIK | SVS crest appears on certificates and reports. Colours and fonts are proposals: match the navy to the school's brand guidelines before printing.

1 · Programme mark

A lens over the field notebook

A magnifying lens on graph-paper grid: looking closely, measuring carefully. The yellow cross-hair is the moment of noticing. The serif wordmark echoes the school's own lettering, so the sub-brand sits naturally beside HIK | SVS.

  • White vinegar, 20 ml
  • Water, 50 ml
  • Spotting tile or white lids
  • Droppers × 2, magnifying glass
