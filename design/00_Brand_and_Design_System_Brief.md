## [Paste into Claude Design] Part 1: Brand

Create a brand identity for a school science enrichment programme.

**Name:** Think Like a Scientist
**Chinese name:** 像科学家一样思考
**Audience:** students aged 11 to 14 in a bilingual (Chinese and English) school in China. English is their second language. Secondary audiences: teachers, school principals and parents.
**What it is:** a ten-week, project-based science programme outside the national curriculum, with no exams. It exists to spark curiosity, teach scientific and critical thinking, and build confidence with scientific English.
**Personality:** curious, hands-on, clever without being childish, a little playful, trustworthy. It should feel like a real scientist's field notebook, not a cartoon and not a corporate brochure.
**Avoid:** generic "school science" clichés (bubbling flasks everywhere, atoms as decoration), childish cartoon styling, neon gradients.

**Deliverables for the brand:**
1. A programme wordmark/logo that works in English alone, Chinese alone, and both together (English above Chinese).
2. Three series sub-brands that clearly belong to the same family, each with its own colour and icon:
   - **Year 7: Ice Engineers | 冰雪工程师.** Theme: winter, ice, building and testing structures. Suggested colour family: cool ice blue. Icon idea: an ice crystal or ice block.
   - **Year 8: Science Detectives | 科学侦探.** Theme: forensic science, solving a mystery with evidence. Suggested colour family: warm amber. Icon idea: a magnifying glass or fingerprint.
   - **Year 9: Claim Busters | 真相调查员.** Theme: testing claims, evidence, critical thinking. Suggested colour family: plum or violet. Icon idea: a speech bubble with a tick, or a question mark becoming a tick.
3. A colour palette: one neutral ink, one paper/background, the three series colours, and one highlight colour for warnings and handover marks. All text colours must meet accessibility contrast.
4. Typography: a Latin font and a matching Simplified Chinese font that sit well together at the same size (for example the Noto Sans / Noto Sans SC family, or a similar pairing). A characterful display face may be used for titles only.
5. A small icon set (see Part 2).

**Hard requirements:**
- **Everything must also work in black and white.** Most worksheets are printed in greyscale on A4. The series must be distinguishable by icon and pattern, not by colour alone.
- Bilingual layouts must give English and Chinese a clear, consistent hierarchy: English primary, Chinese secondary in a lighter weight, never crammed.
- Leave an optional space for a school crest on certificates and reports (placeholder only, no real crest).

## [Paste into Claude Design] Part 2: Design system components

Using the Think Like a Scientist brand, create these reusable templates and components.

**Icon set**
- Five lesson phases: Do Now 课前任务, Hook 引入, Learn 新知, Do 实践, Think and Reflect 思辨与反思
- Three tiers: Core 基础 (one filled dot), Stretch 提高 (two), Challenge 挑战 (three). Must read in greyscale.
- "My thinking: any language, 可用中文" (a thought bubble)
- Safety (goggles, gloves, hot, cold, no eating)
- Five group roles: Lead Scientist 首席科学家, Measurer 测量员, Recorder 记录员, Safety Officer 安全员, Reporter 汇报员

**Slide template (16:9)**
- Title in English with Chinese beneath in a lighter weight.
- Lesson-phase tag in the same corner on every slide.
- A vocabulary panel (English | 中文, up to 6 words) fixed in the same place on every slide that has key words.
- Series colour and icon used as an accent.
- Layouts needed: title slide; Do Now; Hook (one big question or image); Learn (diagram plus text); Do (step-by-step instructions with picture steps); Think (discussion prompt with sentence frames); exit ticket; safety slide (fully bilingual, high visibility).
- No more than about 30 English words per slide.

**Worksheet template (A4 portrait, greyscale-safe)**
Fixed order on every worksheet:
1. Header: title EN / 中文, series icon, week number, Name / Class / Date
2. Vocabulary panel (English | 中文)
3. Core section (tier icon)
4. Stretch section (tier icon)
5. Challenge section (tier icon)
6. "My thinking" box, dashed border, any language allowed
7. Fold-under word bank at the foot of the page, with a dashed fold line
8. Optional exit ticket strip with a cut line
Minimum 12 pt body text (14 pt for Year 7 Core). Generous writing lines. Grids for graphs.

**Other templates**
- Kit card (half A4, landscape): per-group kit list, teacher prep, hazards with English and 中文 lines, clean-up.
- Scientist's Logbook page (A5): What I did 我做了什么 / What I found 我发现了什么 / New words 新词汇 / A question I still have 我还想知道.
- Group role cards (business-card size, one per role).
- Certificate (A4 landscape): series sub-brand, student name, award level (Gold / Silver / Bronze / Participation, plus "Most Improved Scientist"), optional crest placeholder.
- One-page skills report (A4 portrait): six rubric strands with four levels (Beginning 起步, Developing 发展, Secure 达标, Excellent 优秀), skills check Week 1 vs Week 10, teacher comment.
- Poster template (A3) for each series final project.
