# Lesson content file template

Every lesson file follows this structure exactly, so Claude Design (and the incoming teacher) always find things in the same place. Each section marked **[Paste into Claude Design]** is self-contained: it can be pasted on its own, after the design-system brief, and produce a finished output.

House rules for all content:
- British English. No em dashes or en dashes anywhere (use commas, colons, "to").
- 40-minute lesson. Standard phases: Do Now 4, Hook 3, Learn 7, Do 18, Think and Reflect 8. Week 1 and Week 5 adjust for assessment (state the adjusted timings).
- Key vocabulary always shown English | 中文. Instructions in English with key words glossed. Safety lines in full in both languages.
- 6 to 10 key words per lesson. No more than about 30 English words of on-slide text per slide.
- Tiers: Core (all students), Stretch, Challenge. Tiers are not ability groups.
- Chinese text is marked for a native-speaker check in the lesson file header; do not translate whole paragraphs.
- Fictional names only. No real staff or students.

```
# [Year] W[nn] | [English title] | [中文标题]

Series: [series name 中文] | Week [n] of 10 | 40 minutes | Phase: Skills / Checkpoint / Project
Chinese text status: draft, needs native-speaker check
```

## 1. Lesson plan (one page, for the teacher)

- **Big idea:** one sentence.
- **Learning goals:** 2 "I can" statements.
- **Key words:** table: English | 中文 | Student-friendly meaning (simple English).
- **Timeline:** table: Phase | Min | Teacher does | Students do | Resource (slide numbers, worksheet section).
- **Tiers:** what Core, Stretch and Challenge each ask.
- **Language support:** sentence frames used, where Chinese talk is welcome, likely vocabulary traps.
- **Misconceptions to watch for.**
- **Safety:** hazards and controls (or "No practical hazards beyond normal classroom rules").
- **If time is short:** what to cut.
- **Assessment and handover links:** what evidence this lesson produces (exit ticket, logbook line, tracker entry).

## 2. Slides [Paste into Claude Design]

Opening line: "Create a 16:9 slide deck for [Year] Week [n], [title], using the Think Like a Scientist design system, [series] series."

Then for each slide (typically 8 to 12 slides):

```
Slide [n] | Phase tag: [Do Now 课前任务 / Hook 引入 / Learn 新知 / Do 实践 / Think and Reflect 思辨与反思]
Title: [English] / [中文]
On-slide text: [exact text, about 30 English words maximum]
Vocabulary panel: [word | 中文, ...] (or "same as previous")
Visual: [what the image, diagram or layout should show]
Speaker notes: [what the teacher says or asks, 2 to 4 sentences]
```

## 3. Worksheet [Paste into Claude Design]

Opening line: "Create an A4 portrait, black-and-white-printable worksheet for [Year] Week [n], [title], using the Think Like a Scientist worksheet template, [series] series."

Then, in this fixed order, with all text written in full:
1. Header: title EN / 中文, series, week, Name / Class / Date fields
2. Vocabulary panel
3. Core (with any word bank items listed)
4. Stretch
5. Challenge
6. My thinking box (any language, 可用中文): one question
7. Fold-under word bank
8. Exit ticket strip (cut-off at the bottom, or on the back)

Include tables with their exact headings and number of empty rows, and diagrams described precisely.

## 4. Answer key (for the teacher)

Model answers for every question, including acceptable alternatives and a Secure-level example for open questions.

## 5. Kit card [Paste into Claude Design]

Opening line: "Create a half-A4 kit card for [Year] Week [n] using the Think Like a Scientist kit card template."

- Per group: item, quantity
- Teacher prep before the lesson (with how long it takes)
- Hazards and controls, each as English line + 中文 line
- Clean-up

## 6. Other printables [Paste into Claude Design]

Any cards, puzzles, case files, templates or posters the lesson needs, with all text and layout described in full, each with its own opening line ("Create a ...") and print spec (size, copies per class).

## 7. Notes for the incoming teacher

Two to four lines: what to watch, what students found hard in trial (leave blank for now), links to next week.
