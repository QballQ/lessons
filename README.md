# Think Like a Scientist | 像科学家一样思考

Science enrichment programme for the bilingual domestic stream, Years 7 to 9. Ten weeks per year group, project-based, low-equipment, bilingual vocabulary, with a final assessment and a mid-series handover to an incoming teacher.

## Status

Stage 2: lesson content for Weeks 1 to 5 (all years) and the assessment pack, ready for Claude Design. Confirmed: Year 8 Science Detectives, Year 9 Claim Busters, 40-minute lessons. Freezer to be confirmed by Week 4 (Plan A / Plan B in the outline).
