# Think Like a Scientist | 像科学家一样思考

Science enrichment programme for the bilingual domestic stream, Years 7 to 9. Ten weeks per year group, project-based, low-equipment, bilingual vocabulary, with a final assessment and a mid-series handover to an incoming teacher.

## Status

Stage 4: language access pass for Years 7 and 8 (October 2026). Feedback from the Year 7 and Year 8 classes was that learning goals, instructions and questions in English only were not accessible across a range from nearly no English to conversational English. The Year 7 and Year 8 files have been reworked under `00_Language_Support/Language_Access_Policy.md`: goals, instructions, questions, frames, labels and word banks bilingual; Learn content and reading texts with a Chinese key idea or gist; a Today slide in every lesson; a language support pack (instruction words key, classroom language card, frame bank, role cards, dictionary booklets). Year 9 is unchanged for now. Before Week 1: every picture description and printable label that a design tool could have printed in English only now carries its Chinese (checked file by file by a second and third agent), and the Year 7 and Year 8 skills checks carry the audit's language corrections, made before the first printing.

Stage 3 (complete): all content written (Weeks 1 to 10, all years), assessment pack, Teacher Guide and tracker. Design in progress in Claude Design; every Year 7 and 8 deck and printables set was built on 2 and 3 October, before the language pass, so Weeks 1 to 10 must be rebuilt from the updated files, Week 1 first. Week 1 has not yet been taught. Confirmed: Year 8 Science Detectives, Year 9 Claim Busters, 40-minute lessons. Freezer to be confirmed by Week 4 (Plan A / Plan B in the outline).
