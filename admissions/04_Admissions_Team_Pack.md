# Admissions team pack: running an assessment visit

This is the simple pack for the admissions team and the assessors. It says what to print, what happens when, who does what, and where every mark goes. The full guide (`00_Admissions_Guide`) and the evidence documents (`01` to `03`) stay as the background; you do not need them on the day.

## 1. The visit in one page

**Three kinds of paper, so that nothing is printed twice.**

| Kind | What it is | When you print it |
|---|---|---|
| **Print once** (kept in a folder, laminated where marked) | The **assessor instructions** for the year group; the **interviewer guide** for the phase; `Sentence_Reading_Guide`; the reading cards (laminate); the Early Reading Screen guide and cards (Years 1 to 3); the **listening recording** (a sound file, nothing to print) | Once for the room, then reuse. No student name; nobody writes on them |
| **Print for each candidate** (the candidate pack, `pdf/Candidate_Packs/`) | The **student paper** (or booklet); the **grading sheet**; the **Reading front sheet**; the **Interview sheet**; the **parent conversation record**; the **final mark sheet**. Years 1 to 3 also have the Early Reading Screen record sheets | One pack per candidate, double-sided |
| **Sent beforehand** | The previous homeroom teacher reference (English and Chinese) | When the family has given consent |

**Who does what.**

| Role | Does |
|---|---|
| **Admissions officer** | Collects documents and consent, books the visit and the interview slot, prints the candidate pack, welcomes the family, hands the interviewer the sheets, files everything, enters the results, communicates the decision |
| **Assessor** (a teacher) | Runs the written and spoken tasks (Section 3), marks the paper, completes the **grading sheet**, fills in Part 1 of the **Reading front sheet** and hands it over |
| **Interviewer** (usually the principal; the homeroom teacher or the assessor in Years 1 to 3) | Runs the sentence reading (Years 4 to 10) and the interview, completes the **Reading front sheet** (Parts 2 and 3) and the **Interview sheet** |
| **Head of section** | Reads the final mark sheet and confirms the recommendation. The Designated Safeguarding Lead gets anything worrying the same day |

**The visit, step by step.**

| Step | Who | What | Paper |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Admissions | Family arrives. Welcome, show where parents wait. Parent conversation starts with another member of staff (about 15 minutes) | Parent conversation record |
| 2 | Assessor | Runs the sections in Section 3 (the day sheet for the year group) | Student paper, grading sheet |
| 3 | Assessor | Marks the paper, fills in the grading sheet and Part 1 of the Reading front sheet | Grading sheet, Reading front sheet |
| 4 | Admissions | Gives the interviewer the Reading front sheet and the Interview sheet (Section 5) | |
| 5 | Interviewer | Sentence reading and interview, then completes both sheets and returns them | Reading front sheet, Interview sheet |
| 6 | Admissions, head of section | Thanks the family: **no results are given on the day**. Final mark sheet within two working days (Section 6) | Final mark sheet |

## 2. Before the visit

**A week before**
- [[tick]] Application, passport or ID page, school reports for the last two years and signed consent are in. Homeroom teacher reference sent.
- [[tick]] Visit booked (mornings are best for young children). Family told how long it takes, to bring glasses or hearing aids, and asked about access needs.
- [[tick]] Assessor, interviewer (check the principal's diary) and a second adult for young children are booked.

**The day before**
- [[tick]] Candidate pack printed double-sided (`pdf/Candidate_Packs/Year_NN_Candidate_Pack_v3.1.pdf`; the table in Section 8 says what is in it). Years 1 to 3: print only the Early Reading Screen passage sheets you expect to use.
- [[tick]] Room set up: quiet and bright; a low table for Years 1 to 3; a clock; pencils, rubbers, a ruler from Year 3; counters (Years 1 and 2); crayons (Years 1 and 2) or coloured pencils (Year 3); a calculator for each student in Year 9 and 10 maths Section C.
- [[tick]] The assessor's folder is in the room: the assessor instructions, the sentence reading guide and card, and for Years 1 to 3 the Early Reading Screen guide, cards and levelled books.
- [[tick]] **Listening recording** copied to the laptop or phone and the speaker tested (Section 4).

**On the day**
- [[tick]] Candidate number written on every sheet of the pack (the student paper carries the name).
- [[tick]] Door open or a second adult in the room for young children. Anything worrying goes to the Designated Safeguarding Lead the same day.

## 3. Day sheets

Each sheet is the timetable for one phase. Each step has a section in the **assessor instructions** for the year group: the exact words to say, the answers and the marks are there. **Pace of speech:** speak slowly and clearly, in short phrases, with the youngest (Years 1 to 3) and steadily and clearly with the older students, with a pause after each sentence that is longer the younger the student (the recordings are paced this way). Use the same pace for every candidate in a year group.

### Years 1 to 3 (about 46 to 54 minutes, 1:1, play-based)

| Step | Minutes Y1 / Y2 / Y3 | Section | The child | You | Record on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 / 1 / 2 | Welcome | Says their name and age | Smile; sit beside the child; write the name on the booklet cover; show where the parent waits | |
| 2 | 4 / 4 / 4 | Picture talk | Looks at the picture, answers the prompts | Give the prompts in order, wait about 5 seconds, write down the child's own words. Stop after three prompts with no answer | Grading sheet, Section 1 |
| 3 | 5 / 5 / 4 | Listening game | Points, draws, colours | **Play the recording.** Watch the child, not the picture. Mark each item 1 or 0 as you go. If asked, go back and play the item once more (tick R). Stop after three 0s in a row | Grading sheet, Section 2 |
| 4 | 12 / 12 / 12 | Reading: Early Reading Screen, then sentence reading | Reads letter sounds, words and short stories aloud, then sentences | Start at the point for the year (Early Reading Screen guide). Then sentence reading from Level 1 (Years 1 and 2) or Level 2 (Year 3) with `Sentence_Reading_Guide` | Early Reading Screen record (Sheet 13); Reading front sheet, Part 2 |
| 5 | 2 / 2 / 3 to 5 | Movement break | Stretches, has water | Do the actions with the child | |
| 6 | 12 / 15 / 18 | Maths | Counts, points, shows with counters, writes | Read every question as written; repeat or show with counters if asked. Write 1 or 0 in the mark box on the child's page; write NT or L beside it | Mark boxes in the booklet; grading sheet |
| 7 | 5 / 6 / 8 | Writing | Writes their name, dictated words, a sentence or a picture story (by year) | Dictate clearly at the year's pace. Do not spell or write for the child | Grading sheet |
| 8 | 6 / 6 / 8 | Play-based interview and goodbye | Draws and talks | Interviewer (often you): `Interviewer_Guide_Y1_Y2` or `_Y3_Y4`. Do not give results. Say something specific that went well | Interview sheet |
| | **About 46 / 51 / 54** | | | | Then: grading sheet, Reading front sheet Part 1 and 3, final mark sheet |

Years 1 to 3: the assessor runs the sentence reading as well as the Early Reading Screen, so the Reading front sheet stays with the assessor and Parts 1 to 3 are completed together; the interviewer only needs the Interview sheet. Year 2: listening, maths and writing may be done with two children together (assessor instructions, Section 1).

## 4. The listening recordings

Every year group has a recording made from the scripts, so that every candidate hears the same voices, pace and pauses. It replaces the assessor reading the script. Play it on a speaker (not headphones) in a quiet room. Do the volume check before the student arrives. The files are in `audio/v3.1/YNN/`; each folder has a `READ_ME_FIRST.txt` with the tracks and, for Years 1 to 3, the start time of every item.

| Year | Play this | Length | Voice speed (words a minute) | Also |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Y01_Listening_Full_v3.1.mp3` | 4:35 | 185 | start time of every item in the read-me |
| 2 | `Y02_Listening_Full_v3.1.mp3` | 4:40 | 185 | start time of every item in the read-me |
| 3 | `Y03_Listening_Full_v3.1.mp3` | 4:10 | 190 | start time of every item in the read-me |
| 4 | `Y04_Listening_Full_v3.1.mp3` | 6:41 | 165 |  |
| 5 | `Y05_Listening_Full_v3.1.mp3` | 5:50 | 175 |  |
| 6 | `Y06_Listening_Full_v3.1.mp3` | 7:02 | 170 |  |
| 7 | `Y07_Listening_Full_v3.1.mp3` | 13:50 | 200 | Part A and Part B as separate tracks (if you may stop after Part A) |
| 8 | `Y08_Listening_Full_v3.1.mp3` | 14:53 | 180 | Part A and Part B as separate tracks (if you may stop after Part A) |
| 9 | `Y09_Listening_Full_v3.1.mp3` | 14:33 | 180 | Part A and Part B as separate tracks (if you may stop after Part A) |
| 10 | `Y10_Listening_Full_v3.1.mp3` | 15:59 | 185 | Part A and Part B as separate tracks (if you may stop after Part A) |

**How to play it**
1. Student paper (or booklet) open at the right page, pencil ready, grading sheet in front of you.
2. Press play. **Do not pause or repeat** any part of the listening, except for the repeat rule in Years 1 to 3 (go back to the start of that item, play it once, tick R).
3. Mark the paper after the recording. Years 1 to 3: mark each item 1 or 0 as you go.
4. If the recording cannot be played, read the script live from the assessor instructions, at the pace for the year group in Section 3. Never mix the two in one test.

**The pauses are longer for younger students.** The voice speed is the same within each phase (0.85 of the voice's normal speed in Years 1 to 3 and Year 10, 0.9 in Years 4 to 9). The pause after each sentence is about a second in Years 1 to 3, about three quarters of a second in Years 4 to 6 and about half a second in Years 7 to 10. The voices are offline text-to-speech in British English. In two-person scripts the speakers have different voices, so the recording does not say their names.

## 5. Handing over to the interviewer

The interviewer is usually the principal. When you book the interview, give the interviewer one **interview pack** for each candidate, **before** the interview starts:

| Give | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Reading front sheet** (2 pages) | Part 1 is already filled in by the assessor: the reading levels so far (Early Reading Screen, or the reading paper marks), the reading band so far, and where the sentence reading starts. The interviewer fills in Parts 2 and 3 |
| **Interview sheet** (2 pages) | Speaking and listening bands, key things the student said, the suitability ratings, follow-ups, the interviewer's comment |

The interviewer's own folder, kept from one candidate to the next (nothing to print again): `Interviewer_Guide_...` for the phase (the questions and descriptors), `Sentence_Reading_Guide` (the rules, the sentences and the meaning questions) and the laminated sentence reading card.

**How it goes.** 1. The assessor completes Part 1 of the Reading front sheet and gives both sheets to the interviewer (write who gave it and when, in Part 1). 2. The interviewer starts the interview with the sentence reading, from the start level printed on the front sheet (Level 1 if the assessor ticked "new to English"). 3. The interviewer asks the questions from the guide, then completes the Interview sheet while the memory is fresh. 4. Both sheets come back to admissions the same day, with a word to the assessor about anything unexpected. 5. Admissions clips the two sheets to the grading sheet and the parent conversation record.

## 6. After the visit

**The same day**
- Assessor: mark the paper; grading sheet complete, signed; Reading front sheet Part 1 complete.
- Interviewer: Reading front sheet Parts 2 and 3, and the Interview sheet, complete.
- Any safeguarding concern: told to the Designated Safeguarding Lead.

**Within two working days: the final mark sheet** (`forms/Candidate_Summary_and_Decision`). Copy the scores from the grading sheet, the Reading front sheet and the Interview sheet, give the overall English band, tick one recommendation, write the support plan, and add the comments and the feedback for the family. The head of section confirms it. Admissions tells the family (start with the child's strengths).

**Entering it in the system.** One entry for each candidate, in this order, from the final mark sheet:

| Field | From |
|---|---|
| Candidate number, name, date of birth, year group applied for, paper version sat | Final mark sheet, top |
| Listening, speaking, reading, reading aloud (indicative), writing, overall English: a band A to E for each | Final mark sheet, Section 1 |
| Sentence reading points out of 72, reading age | Reading front sheet, Part 3 |
| Maths score, band 1 to 4, number problems and word problems sub-scores; science score and band (Years 5 to 10) | Grading sheet, final mark sheet Section 2 |
| Suitability ratings (any 1 flagged), follow-ups | Interview sheet, final mark sheet Section 3 |
| Recommendation, support plan, review date | Final mark sheet, Sections 4 and 5 |
| Date told to the family | Final mark sheet, sign-off |

**Filing.** Keep the grading sheet, Reading front sheet, Interview sheet, parent conversation record and the marked student paper together with the final mark sheet, securely, for the period the school's data policy sets. Papers are confidential: do not let families photograph them or take them home.

## 7. The rules on one page

| Rule | English sections (listening, reading, writing, sentence reading) | Maths and science |
|---|---|---|
| Reading aloud | Only what the script says. Do not read the reading texts, explain words or translate. An instruction may be repeated in English | Any question may be read aloud in English, as often as asked, and you may point to the picture |
| Chinese | Not used by staff | A Chinese-speaking colleague may say the question **once**. Mark **L**. The mark still counts |
| Answers | In English only. An answer in Chinese scores 0 | In English only: numbers, ticks, drawings and working count. A Chinese answer or explanation scores 0 |

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **NT** | Not yet taught: the student says they have not learnt it. Scores 0; list the topics on the grading sheet |
| **L** | A Chinese-speaking colleague said a maths or science question once in Chinese |
| **R** | A listening instruction was repeated once (Years 1 to 3) |

**Always:** the student may stop any section when they have done what they can. Never press a distressed child: break or stop, and write it on the grading sheet. Praise effort, never correctness. Give no results on the day. Use the same papers, timings and pace for every candidate in a year group.

**If something goes wrong.** The recording will not play: read the script live at the year's pace (Section 4). A student is silent: smile, wait, move on; pointing tasks still work. A student stops early: record where and why. A parent wants to stay with a young child: they may sit quietly at the back; note it. A sheet is lost: reprint it from the candidate pack PDF; the version is on every page.
