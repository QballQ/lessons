# Version register

Which version of the papers each year group uses, and what each version contains. Every paper and booklet shows its version on the front page and in the footer of every page. **Mark a paper only with the booklet of the same version**: the question numbers, marks and band guides differ.

## Which version to use

| Year group | Version | Candidate pack to print (`pdf/Candidate_Packs/`) |
|---|---|---|
| Years 1 to 6 | **v1** | `Year_NN_Candidate_Pack.pdf` |
| Years 7 to 9 | **v2** | `Year_NN_Candidate_Pack_v2.pdf` |
| Year 10 | **v2**, or **v3** for the trial | `Year_10_Candidate_Pack_v2.pdf` or `Year_10_Candidate_Pack_v3.pdf` |

## The versions

| Version | Date | Year groups | What it contains | Status | Files |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **v1** | October 2026 | 1 to 10 | The first version: listening (8 or 10 marks), reading, writing, maths with number problems and word problems, science from Year 5, interview | Current for Years 1 to 6. Superseded by v2 for Years 7 to 10 (kept for reference) | `YNN_Student_Paper` (Years 1 to 3: `YNN_Student_Booklet`), `YNN_Assessor_Booklet`; Years 7 to 10 packs in `v1_superseded/` |
| **v2** | October 2026 | 7 to 10 | Harder listening (Part B, an academic talk; 16 marks), harder reading (a new Part C; 24 marks), maths aimed at the higher end (fluency, word questions and applied problems; calculator in Section C only). Writing, science and the interview as v1 | Current for Years 7 to 10 | `YNN_Student_Paper_v2`, `YNN_Assessor_Booklet_v2` |
| **v3** | 20 October 2026 | 10 only | The v1 Year 10 papers (reading, writing, maths, science) with the v2 listening (Parts A and B, 16 marks, v2 band guide) | Trial | `Y10_Student_Paper_v3`, `Y10_Assessor_Booklet_v3`; download `download/Year_10_v3.zip` |

Shared by every version: the reading screens (`shared/`), the interview, parent and homeroom teacher forms and the candidate summary (`forms/`).

**Adding a version.** Give it the next number, save its files with the suffix (`_v4` and so on), put the version on the front page and in the footer, and add a row to the table above with the date, the year groups and what changed.
