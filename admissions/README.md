# International Programme Admissions Assessment, Years 1 to 10

Admission assessments for students joining the English-medium international programme, most of them from Chinese mainstream schools or other bilingual schools. For every year group from Year 1 to Year 12 the pack measures the student's **level of English** (listening, speaking, reading and writing), their **maths** (number problems, word problems, and problems with diagrams and pictures), their **science** from Year 5, and, through an interview and a parent conversation, **who they are, their future plans and aspirations, and their suitability** for the programme. Each assessment takes about 46 minutes in Year 1, rising to about 95 minutes in Years 7 to 10 and about 2 hours in Years 11 and 12 (where a cognitive ability screen and optional IGCSE-level science papers are added).

The student papers are in English only and are designed to be accessible: large clear type, pictures and diagrams, short sentences, worked examples, easy questions first.

**Running a visit? Start with `04_Admissions_Team_Pack`** (the simple pack for the admissions team: what to print, day sheets with the minutes for each section, the listening recordings, the handover to the interviewer, filing). **`00_Admissions_Guide`** is the full overview and the evidence behind it (the PDFs are in `pdf/`). **`tracker/`** has the admissions tracker workbook: a sheet for each year group that feeds a dashboard of stages, levels, decisions and support.

## What to print

Papers are split into **reusable instructions** (printed once, no student name, nothing written on them) and **short sheets for each candidate**. Ready-to-print PDFs are in `pdf/`.

**Every year group uses version 3.1** (October 2026; `03_Version_Register` says what each version contains).

- **For each candidate:** `pdf/Candidate_Packs/Year_05_Candidate_Pack_v3.1.pdf` (and so on) has everything printed for one candidate, in order: the student paper or booklet, the **grading sheet**, (Years 1 to 3) the Early Reading Screen record sheets, the **Reading front sheet**, the **Interview sheet**, the parent conversation record, the **final mark sheet** and the **assessment result and report** (the front cover that goes with the marked papers). Print it double-sided: every part starts on a new sheet. For Years 1 to 3, print only the Early Reading Screen passage sheets you expect to use.
- **Once for the room:** `pdf/Print_Once/Year_05_Assessor_Folder_v3.1.pdf` has the assessor instructions, the interviewer guide for the phase, the sentence reading guide and (Years 1 to 3) the Early Reading Screen guide. `pdf/Print_Once/Reading_Cards_to_Laminate.pdf` has the cards students read from.
- **Sent before the visit:** the homeroom teacher reference (`pdf/forms/`).
- **Listening recordings:** `audio/v3.1/YNN/` has a recording for every year group (nothing to print).
- Packs of earlier versions (v1, v2 and v3) are in `pdf/Candidate_Packs/superseded/`, for reference only. `download/` has the zips: `Admissions_Pack_v3.1.zip` (all the PDFs, organised for the admissions team) the tracker in `7_ADMISSIONS_TRACKER`, and four listening downloads (`Listening_Audio_Years_1_to_6_v3.1.zip`, `Listening_Audio_Years_7_to_8_v3.1.zip`, `Listening_Audio_Years_9_to_10_v3.1.zip` and `Listening_Audio_Years_11_to_12_v3.1.zip`).

All student papers are in English only, and every answer must be in English: an answer in Chinese scores 0. In maths and science the assessor may read any question aloud in English; if a Chinese-speaking colleague is present, they may say a question once in Chinese (marked L), so that a maths or science result is not hidden by English.

## Editing and rebuilding

The Markdown files are the sources. After editing one, rebuild its PDF:

```
python3 admissions/tools/build.py admissions/Y05/Y05_Student_Paper_v3.1.md
```

`python3 admissions/tools/build.py` with no file names rebuilds everything. It needs Python 3 with `markdown` and `pikepdf`, Node with Playwright and Chromium, and `pdftoppm` for page previews. After rebuilding, `python3 admissions/tools/make_packs.py` remakes the version 3.1 candidate packs and the print-once assessor folders. `python3 admissions/tools/make_listening_audio.py` (see its header) remakes the listening recordings. `python3 admissions/tools/make_tracker.py --recalc` remakes the two tracker workbooks (it needs `openpyxl` and LibreOffice; the maximum marks in it are copied from the grading sheets, so change them there first if a paper changes). The sentence reading screen is generated from `tools/make_sentence_screen.py`, and the early reading screen from `tools/early_reading_screen/` (`ers_data.py` holds the words and passages), so edit them there and regenerate. How the papers are written (house rules, shortcodes, diagram rules) is in `tools/STYLE_GUIDE.md`; the design of every year group's assessment is in `tools/BLUEPRINT.md`.

## Status

The bands are provisional: the pack has not yet been standardised. After the first 20 to 30 admissions, compare the bands with how students are doing after a term and adjust (guide, Section 10).
