# Student interview and suitability record: Years 3 and 4

[[namebox:Candidate name,Year group applied for,Date,Interviewer,Others present (for example receiving homeroom teacher; Chinese-speaking colleague)]]

**About 8 minutes in Year 3 and 12 minutes in Year 4, 1:1.** The interview is held **in English**, as a friendly chat. It has three jobs: to hear the child's spoken English, to get to know the child (likes, school, friends, **hopes for the future**), and to judge their **suitability** for the international programme. In Year 3 the sentence reading is done with the Early Reading Screen; in Year 4 the interview opens with the Graded Sentence Reading Screen (start Level 3; record on `shared/Sentence_Reading_Record.md`).

**How to ask.** Sit beside the child. Have paper and crayons ready: some children talk more easily while drawing ("Draw something you love doing, and tell me about it"). Ask the main question first. If the child struggles, use the **easier** prompt (a yes or no, a choice, or pointing). If they answer easily, use the **stretch** prompt. Wait at least five seconds before rephrasing. Answers must be in English.

**Safeguarding.** Keep the door open or another adult present. If a child tells you something that worries you, do not promise to keep it secret; listen, write down their words, and pass it to the Designated Safeguarding Lead the same day.

## 3. Speaking band

Circle the band that fits best, for a child of this age.

| Band | What you hear |
|---|---|
| **A** New to English | No English yet, or a few single words ("yes", "cat"). Uses Chinese, pointing or silence. Understands very little without pictures or gesture. |
| **B** Early acquisition | Single words, short phrases and some simple sentences ("I like football"). Needs the easier prompts. Understands slow, simple questions with support. |
| **C** Developing competence | Answers in short sentences and can say a little more with prompting ("I like football because it is fun"). Errors are common; meaning is usually clear. |
| **D** Competent | Talks easily, joins ideas, tells a little story or explains, with occasional errors. Understands nearly everything said at a normal pace. |
| **E** Fluent | Speaks as a fluent English-speaking child of the same age would. |
