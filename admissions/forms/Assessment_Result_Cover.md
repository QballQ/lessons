## About this report {style="break-before: page"}

This report goes with the student's marked papers. It was made from the grading sheet, the reading and interview sheets and the parent conversation. The assessment was in English only, so the English bands show how far the student's English has come, and maths and science show what the student can do when the English is not the barrier. Some topics are taught in a different order in Chinese schools: where a student has not yet been taught a topic it scores 0 and the report says so.

#### The English bands

| Band | Listening | Speaking | Reading | Writing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** New to English | Understands little or no English; may follow a routine instruction with gesture. | Silent, or single words and memorised phrases; may use Chinese. | May know some letter names or sounds; reads few or no words. | Copies, writes their name, or writes isolated words; may write in Chinese. |
| **B** Early acquisition | Understands simple, slow questions and instructions on familiar topics, with support. | Short phrases and simple sentences about familiar things; frequent errors. | Decodes simple words and short sentences; understands very simple texts with pictures. | Short simple sentences with familiar words; frequent errors in grammar and spelling; meaning mostly clear in short pieces. |
| **C** Developing competence | Follows most everyday talk and simple explanations; misses detail and implied meaning. | Connected sentences with "because", "but", "so"; errors common but meaning usually clear; everyday vocabulary. | Reads simple texts two or more years below age level with understanding; struggles with age-level texts. | A short connected piece; some joined sentences; errors frequent but rarely block meaning; basic punctuation. |
| **D** Competent | Follows most classroom talk at normal pace, including some implied meaning. | Speaks at length, explains and gives examples; occasional errors. | Reads age-level texts with some difficulty in vocabulary and inference. | Organised writing with a range of sentences; generally accurate with occasional errors; varied vocabulary. |
| **E** Fluent | Understands as a fluent speaker of the same age does. | Fluent and accurate for age. | Reads age-level texts as a fluent reader of the same age does. | Writing comparable to a fluent writer of the same age. |

**Reading age** is an indicative figure from a short reading-aloud screen: 5 years 0 months plus 2 months for every point. It is a guide, not a standardised test.

**Maths and science bands** compare the score with what is expected for the year group: 1 Well below expected, 2 Working towards, 3 Expected, 4 Greater depth. The papers for older students are harder, so their boundaries are lower.
