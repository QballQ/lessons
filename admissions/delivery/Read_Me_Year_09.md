# Year 9 admission assessment: read me first

**This folder holds everything for a Year 9 student.** Print from folder **1** for each student, print folder **2** once and keep it in the room, play the recording in folder **3**, and send folder **4** to the student's current school before the visit. The visit takes **about 94 minutes**; the parent conversation (about 15 minutes) runs at the same time with another member of staff. **No results are given on the day.**

#### The visit, in order

| Step | What happens | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome | 2 |
| 2 | Listening, Parts A and B: play the Full recording | 14 |
| 3 | Reading paper (Parts A and B) | 15 |
| 4 | Writing (one task of two) | 10 |
| 5 | Break | 5 |
| 6 | Maths (calculator in Section C only, Years 9 and 10) | 25 |
| 7 | Science | 8 |
| 8 | Interview, opening with sentence reading (Level 8) | 15 |

#### Folder 1: print one of each for every student (double-sided)

| File | Pages | Hand to | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Visit checklist | 2 | Admissions keeps it and ticks it | before, during and after the visit |
| 2 Student paper Year 9 | 21 | the assessor | the student writes in it |
| 3 Grading sheet Year 9 | 2 | the assessor | marks go here |
| 4 Reading front sheet | 2 | the assessor, then the interviewer | the assessor fills in Part 1 and passes it on |
| 5 Interview sheet Years 7 to 10 | 2 | the interviewer | before the interview |
| 6 Parent conversation record | 3 | the staff member who talks to the parents | during the visit |
| 7 Final mark sheet | 3 | admissions | within two working days |
| 8 Assessment result and report | 2 | admissions | after the decision is confirmed; given to the family with the marked papers |

Write the candidate number on every sheet. Years 1 to 3 booklets print best in colour.

#### Folder 2: print once and keep in the room (nothing is written on these)

| File | Pages | For |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Day sheet, pin up | 1 | the room |
| 2 Assessor instructions Year 9 | 14 | the assessor |
| 3 Interviewer guide Years 7 to 10 | 3 | the interviewer |
| 4 Sentence reading guide | 6 | the interviewer (Years 1 to 3: the assessor) |
| 5 Sentence reading card, laminate | 4 | the interviewer (Years 1 to 3: the assessor) |

#### Folder 3: the listening recording

Play **Year 9 listening.mp3** (13:57 minutes) on a speaker, in a quiet room. Check the volume before the student arrives. Do not pause it: each part gives one minute to read the questions, plays the text twice and gives time to finish. Use the two **Part A only** and **Part B only** files instead if you may stop after Part A (a student with 2 or fewer in Part A need not do Part B). If the recording cannot be played, the assessor reads the script from the assessor instructions.

#### Folder 4: before the visit

Send the **homeroom teacher reference** to the student's current school once the family has given consent (it is in English and Chinese).

#### After the visit

1. The same day: the assessor signs the grading sheet; the interviewer completes the Reading front sheet and the Interview sheet; all sheets come back to admissions. Anything worrying goes to the Designated Safeguarding Lead the same day.
2. Within two working days: the **final mark sheet** is completed from those sheets and the Head of School or Deputy confirms the decision.
3. Admissions enters the student in the **tracker** (Office folder), completes the **assessment result and report**, tells the family (starting with the student's strengths) and gives the report with the marked papers. File everything together.

**The three rules.** English sections: staff read only the script, never the texts, and never translate. Maths and science: any question may be read aloud in English; a Chinese-speaking colleague may say it once in Chinese (mark L). All answers in English: an answer in Chinese scores 0.
