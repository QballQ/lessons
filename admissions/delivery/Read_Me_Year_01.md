# Year 1 admission assessment: read me first

**This folder holds everything for a Year 1 student.** Print from folder **1** for each student, print folder **2** once and keep it in the room, play the recording in folder **3**, and send folder **4** to the student's current school before the visit. The visit takes **about 45 minutes**; the parent conversation (about 15 minutes) runs at the same time with another member of staff. **No results are given on the day.**

#### The visit, in order

| Step | What happens | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome | 1 |
| 2 | Picture talk (speaking) | 4 |
| 3 | Listening game: play the recording | 4 |
| 4 | Reading: Early Reading Screen, then sentence reading | 12 |
| 5 | Writing | 5 |
| 6 | Movement break | 2 |
| 7 | Maths | 12 |
| 8 | Play-based interview and goodbye | 6 |

#### Folder 1: print one of each for every student (double-sided)

| File | Pages | Hand to | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Visit checklist | 2 | Admissions keeps it and ticks it | before, during and after the visit |
| 2 Student booklet Year 1 | 14 | the assessor | the student writes in it |
| 3 Grading sheet Year 1 | 2 | the assessor | marks go here |
| 4 Early Reading Screen record | 18 | the assessor | print only the passage sheets you expect to use |
| 5 Reading front sheet | 2 | the assessor, then the interviewer | the assessor fills in Part 1 and passes it on |
| 6 Interview sheet Years 1 and 2 | 2 | the interviewer | before the interview |

Write the candidate number on every sheet. Years 1 to 3 booklets print best in colour.

#### Folder 3: the listening recording

Play **Year 1 listening.mp3** (4:27 minutes) on a speaker, in a quiet room. Check the volume before the student arrives. It explains the game, gives a practice item, then the 10 instructions with time to respond. Mark each item 1 or 0 on the grading sheet as you go. If the child asks, or clearly did not hear, go back to the start of that item and play it once more (tick R). Stop after three 0s in a row. If the recording cannot be played, the assessor reads the script from the assessor instructions.

#### Folder 4: before the visit

Send the **homeroom teacher reference** to the student's current school once the family has given consent (it is in English and Chinese).

#### After the visit

1. The same day: the assessor signs the grading sheet; the interviewer completes the Reading front sheet and the Interview sheet; all sheets come back to admissions. Anything worrying goes to the Designated Safeguarding Lead the same day.
2. Within two working days: the **final mark sheet** is completed from those sheets and the Head of School or Deputy confirms the decision.
3. Admissions enters the student in the **tracker** (Office folder), completes the **assessment result and report**, tells the family (starting with the student's strengths) and gives the report with the marked papers. File everything together.

**The three rules.** English sections: staff read only the script, never the texts, and never translate. Maths and science: any question may be read aloud in English; a Chinese-speaking colleague may say it once in Chinese (mark L). All answers in English: an answer in Chinese scores 0.
