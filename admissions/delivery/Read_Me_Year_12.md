# Year 12 admission assessment: read me first

**This folder holds everything for a Year 12 student.** Print from folder **1** for each student, print folder **2** once and keep it in the room, play the recording in folder **3**, and send folder **4** to the student's current school before the visit. The visit takes **about 2 hours (plus 15 minutes for each optional science)**; the parent conversation (about 15 minutes) runs at the same time with another member of staff. **No results are given on the day.**

#### The visit, in order

| Step | What happens | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome | 2 |
| 2 | Listening, Parts A and B: play the Full recording | 17 |
| 3 | Reading paper (Parts A and B) | 15 |
| 4 | Writing (one task of two) | 10 |
| 5 | Break | 5 |
| 6 | Maths (calculator in Section C only) | 25 |
| 7 | Core science | 8 |
| 8 | Break | 5 |
| 9 | Cognitive ability screen (strictly timed) | 25 |
| 10 | Optional sciences: only those the student will take | 15 each |
| 11 | Interview, opening with sentence reading (Level 9) | 15 |

#### Folder 1: print one of each for every student (double-sided)

| File | Pages | Hand to | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Visit checklist | 2 | Admissions keeps it and ticks it | before, during and after the visit |
| 2 Student paper Year 12 | 22 | the assessor | the student writes in it |
| 3 Student record Year 12 | 9 | the assessor, then the interviewer, then admissions | one booklet: marks, reading, interview, decision |
| 4 Cognitive ability screen booklet | 14 | the assessor | the student writes in it |
| 5 Optional sciences paper Year 12 | 13 | the assessor | print only the sciences the student will take |
| 6 Parent conversation record | 3 | the staff member who talks to the parents | during the visit |
| 7 Assessment result and report | 2 | admissions | after the decision is confirmed; given to the family with the marked papers |

Write the candidate number on every sheet. Years 1 to 3 booklets print best in colour.

#### Folder 3: the listening recording

Play **Year 12 listening.mp3** (17:25 minutes) on a speaker, in a quiet room. Check the volume before the student arrives. Do not pause it: each part gives one minute to read the questions, plays the text twice and gives time to finish. Use the two **Part A only** and **Part B only** files instead if you may stop after Part A (a student with 2 or fewer in Part A need not do Part B). If the recording cannot be played, the assessor reads the script from the assessor instructions.

#### Folder 4: before the visit

Send the **homeroom teacher reference** to the student's current school once the family has given consent (it is in English and Chinese).

**Years 11 and 12.** The cognitive ability screen is for planning support, never for selection; follow its guide and the times exactly. Give only the optional sciences the student will take (agree them with the family before the day). If the day is too long, the optional sciences and the interview may be on a second visit.

#### After the visit

1. The same day: the assessor completes Part A and the top of Part B of the **student record** and hands it to the interviewer; the interviewer completes Parts B and C and returns it to admissions. Anything worrying goes to the Designated Safeguarding Lead the same day.
2. Within two working days: admissions completes Part D of the student record (the decision) and the Head of School or Deputy confirms it.
3. Admissions enters the student in the **tracker** (Office folder), completes the **assessment result and report**, tells the family (starting with the student's strengths) and gives the report with the marked papers. File everything together.

**The three rules.** English sections: staff read only the script, never the texts, and never translate. Maths and science: any question may be read aloud in English; a Chinese-speaking colleague may say it once in Chinese (mark L). All answers in English: an answer in Chinese scores 0.
