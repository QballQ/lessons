Year 9 Admission Assessment, version 3.1: listening recording

Use with Y09_Student_Paper_v3.1, Y09_Assessor_Instructions_v3.1 (Section 3, Listening) and Y09_Grading_Sheet_v3.1.

TRACKS
  Y09_Listening_Full_v3.1.mp3   14:33 minutes: Part A then Part B
  Y09_Listening_Part_A_v3.1.mp3   7:05 minutes: one part only
  Y09_Listening_Part_B_v3.1.mp3   8:27 minutes: one part only

PACE
  The voice speed is the same for the whole year group; the younger the student, the longer the pauses between sentences. In Year 9 the voice speaks at about 180 words a minute (not counting the pauses), with a pause after every sentence. Counting the pauses, one reading of the script goes at about 135 words a minute: a live reader can use this to check their own pace.

HOW TO USE
  1. Have the student paper open at the Listening pages and a pencil ready. Check the volume before the student
     arrives.
  2. Play the Full track (Part A, then Part B). Do not pause the recording. Each part gives one minute to read the
     questions, plays the script twice (10 seconds apart) and gives time to finish.
  3. Use Part A and Part B as separate tracks if you may need to stop after Part A (stopping rules in the
     instructions, Section 2): play Part A, mark it, then play Part B only if the rule allows.
  4. Where the speakers are two people they have different voices, so the recording does not say their names.
  5. Year 9: in the Full track the minute before Part B is also the time to finish Part A (as in the instructions). The separate Part A track gives its own minute to finish.

VOICES AND VOLUME
  The voices are offline text-to-speech, British English. Play the recording on a speaker at a clear, comfortable
  volume in a quiet room. Mark the paper with the version 3.1 instructions as usual.
