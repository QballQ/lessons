## Year 7 (7A, 7B): Ice Engineers 冰雪工程师

### Brief description of content of the course 课程内容概述

Think Like a Scientist: Ice Engineers. Students learned how scientists work: separating observations from inferences, asking testable questions, and planning fair tests with independent, dependent and control variables. They took repeat measurements, calculated means, spotted anomalies and drew graphs to reach conclusions. They then applied these skills to winter investigations on thermal insulators and on making ice stronger with fibres (composite materials).

像科学家一样思考：冰雪工程师。学生学习科学家的工作方式：区分观察与推断，提出可检验的问题，并用自变量、因变量和控制变量设计公平测试。他们进行重复测量、计算平均值、找出异常值并绘制图表得出结论，再把这些技能用于冬季探究：隔热材料，以及用纤维加固冰块（复合材料）。

## Year 8 (8A, 8B): Science Detectives 科学侦探

### Brief description of content of the course 课程内容概述

Think Like a Scientist: Science Detectives. Students investigated a fictional case using forensic science. They separated observations from inferences, classified fingerprints, separated inks by paper chromatography, identified unknown powders with systematic tests, and used indicators and the pH scale to identify a liquid. They used scatter graphs to estimate height from footprints, planned and carried out their own forensic tests, and weighed the strength of each piece of evidence to reach a conclusion.

像科学家一样思考：科学侦探。学生运用法庭科学调查一个虚构案件：区分观察与推断，给指纹分类，用纸色谱法分离墨水，通过系统检测识别未知粉末，并用指示剂和pH值识别液体。他们用散点图根据脚印估计身高，设计并完成自己的法庭科学检测，并权衡每条证据的强弱得出结论。
