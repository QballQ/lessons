DNA from fruit: worksheet answers答案

Students can answer in Chinese anywhere: mark the science, not the English. "Secure" shows what a full answer looks like.

Core 基础

a)A cell wall 细胞壁 · B cell membrane 细胞膜 · C nucleus 细胞核. Circle the nucleus.If asked: a small amount of DNA is also in the chloroplasts and mitochondria, which is why the question says "most of".
b)All ten ticked. Walk past at steps 4 and 7: the commonest slips are squeezing the filter and shaking the tube.
c)
Squashing DWashing-up liquid BSalt EFilter CCold alcohol A
d)Drawing: alcohol on top, fruit liquid underneath, white material at the line between them (some may float up into the alcohol). All three labelled.
"The DNA looks white and stringy (or cloudy)." "It appeared at the line between the layers (and floated up into the alcohol)." Amount: any honest answer is right.

Stretch 提高

a)To take out the solid bits of fruit, so only the liquid with the DNA goes into the tube.Secure: "The filter removes the cell walls and pulp. The DNA is dissolved in the liquid, so it goes through. Then the DNA is easier to see and is not mixed up with bits of fruit."
b)No. One DNA molecule is far too thin to see (about 2 nanometres wide), even with a school microscope.Secure: "No. We can only see the white strands because millions of DNA strands have clumped together. One strand on its own is much too thin to see."
c)Accept any of: protein, bits of fruit or other cell material, pectin or other carbohydrate from the fruit, a little soap or salt. (RNA is also correct, for a strong student.)

Challenge 挑战

a)Strawberry, because each cell has more sets of chromosomes, so more DNA in every cell.Secure: adds a condition, for example "only if we use the same mass of fruit and the same method", or notes that cell size and water content also matter, so the prediction needs testing.
b)I change: salt or no salt (or the amount of salt). I measure: the amount of DNA, for example the height of the white layer in mm, or a score from 0 (none) to 3 (lots) using photos.
I keep the same: the fruit and how much, the amount of water and washing-up liquid, squashing time, the alcohol (type, amount, how cold), waiting time.Secure: only one thing changes, the measurement is described clearly enough to repeat, and at least three things are kept the same.

My thinking 我的想法

Any thoughtful answer, in either language. Listen for: DNA is a set of instructions. Every living thing uses the same chemical code, but the code is in a different order, so it gives different instructions and builds a different living thing.

Exit ticket 离场小测

1 nucleus 细胞核 · 2 does not dissolve 不溶于 · 3 any honest answer: read these to plan what to recap next time.

Listen out for

"I can see the double helix"No: millions of strands in a clump.
"The alcohol makes the DNA"It was already there. The alcohol makes it come out of the liquid.
"Fruit is not alive, so it has no DNA"DNA is a molecule. It stays in the cells after the fruit is picked.
"The DNA is only in the seeds"Every cell has DNA, including the flesh.
