## Worksheet [Paste into Claude Design]

Create an A4 portrait, black-and-white-printable worksheet, DNA from fruit, using the Think Like a Scientist worksheet template, no series colour (standalone practical). Chinese sits after each English line in the lighter weight.

**1. Header**
DNA from fruit / 从水果中提取DNA | Think Like a Scientist 像科学家一样思考 · Practical 实验 | Name 姓名 ________ Class 班级 ________ Date 日期 ________
Today I can 今天我能:
1. I can follow a method carefully and safely to get DNA out of fruit. 我能安全、仔细地按照实验步骤从水果中提取DNA。
2. I can explain what the washing-up liquid, the salt and the cold alcohol each do. 我能解释洗洁精、盐和冷酒精各自的作用。

**2. Vocabulary panel**
DNA | cell 细胞 | cell wall 细胞壁 | cell membrane 细胞膜 | nucleus 细胞核 | extract 提取 | filter 过滤 | dissolve 溶解 | layer 层

Safety box under the panel: Stay safe 注意安全: Goggles on. Alcohol is flammable and poisonous: never drink or taste it. 戴上护目镜。酒精易燃、有毒：不要喝，也不要尝。

**3. Core 基础 (one dot)**

a) Where is the DNA? Label the plant cell: write cell wall, cell membrane and nucleus in boxes A, B and C. Then circle where most of the DNA is. DNA在哪里？标注植物细胞：在A、B、C框里写上细胞壁、细胞膜和细胞核。然后圈出大部分DNA所在的位置。
(Diagram: a simple plant cell, rounded rectangle with a thick outer line for the cell wall, a thin line just inside it for the cell membrane, a grey oval nucleus, two or three small unlabelled ovals. Leader lines: A to the outer wall, B to the inner membrane, C into the nucleus. Answer lines A, B, C beside it.)

b) Do the practical. Tick each step when you finish it. 做实验。每完成一步就勾选。
(Two columns of tick boxes, steps 1 to 5 left, 6 to 10 right.)
1. Goggles on. 戴上护目镜。
2. Fruit and 4 teaspoons of extraction liquid in the bag. Seal it. 把水果和4茶匙提取液放进袋子，封好。
3. Squash gently for 2 minutes. 轻轻挤压2分钟。
4. Filter it. Wait 5 minutes. Do not squeeze. 过滤。等5分钟。不要挤压。
5. Pour the liquid into the tube, a quarter full. 把液体倒进试管，到四分之一满。
6. Get the cold alcohol from your teacher. 向老师领取冷酒精。
7. Tilt the tube. Pour the alcohol slowly down the side. 倾斜试管，沿管壁慢慢倒入酒精。
8. Wait 3 to 5 minutes. Do not shake. 等3到5分钟。不要摇晃。
9. Twist the stick at the line. Lift out the DNA. 在分界线处转动小棍，挑出DNA。
10. Clean up. Wash your hands. 清理桌面，洗手。

c) What does each one do? Write the letter in the box. 每一样有什么作用？把字母写在框里。

| Box | Step 步骤 | | Job 作用 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Squashing 挤压 | A | makes the DNA come out of the liquid, because DNA does not dissolve in alcohol 让DNA从液体中析出，因为DNA不溶于酒精 |
| ☐ | Washing-up liquid 洗洁精 | B | breaks the membranes round the cell and the nucleus 破坏细胞膜和核膜 |
| ☐ | Salt 盐 | C | removes the solid bits 去除固体残渣 |
| ☐ | Filter 过滤 | D | breaks open the cell walls 打破细胞壁 |
| ☐ | Cold alcohol 冷酒精 | E | helps the DNA strands clump together 帮助DNA聚集在一起 |

d) Draw what you see in your tube. Label the alcohol, the fruit liquid and the DNA. 画出你在试管里看到的东西。标注酒精、水果液体和DNA。
(Empty test tube outline, about 6 cm tall, with three short label lines to its right.)
The DNA looks ________ . DNA看起来________。
It appeared ________ . 它出现在________。
How much DNA did you get? Circle one. 你们得到了多少DNA？圈出一个。 none 没有 / a little 一点 / a lot 很多
Word bank 词库: white 白色 · stringy 丝状的 · cloudy 浑浊 · at the line between the layers 在两层之间的分界线上 · in the alcohol 在酒精里

**4. Stretch 提高 (two dots)**

a) Why do we filter the mixture before we add the alcohol? 为什么在加入酒精之前要先过滤？ (2 lines)

b) Can you see the double helix in your tube? Explain. 你能在试管里看到DNA的双螺旋吗？请解释。 (2 lines)

## Answer key (for the teacher)

Students can answer in Chinese anywhere: mark the science, not the English.

**Core**
- a) A cell wall 细胞壁, B cell membrane 细胞膜, C nucleus 细胞核. Circle the nucleus. If asked: a small amount of DNA is also in the chloroplasts and mitochondria, which is why the question says "most of".
- b) All ten ticked. Walk past at steps 4 and 7: the commonest slips are squeezing the filter and shaking the tube.
- c) Squashing D, washing-up liquid B, salt E, filter C, cold alcohol A.
- d) Drawing: alcohol on top, fruit liquid underneath, white material at the line between them (some may float up into the alcohol), all three labelled. "The DNA looks white and stringy (or cloudy)." "It appeared at the line between the layers (and floated up into the alcohol)." Amount: any honest answer is right.

**Stretch**
- a) To take out the solid bits of fruit, so only the liquid with the DNA goes into the tube. Secure: "The filter removes the cell walls and pulp. The DNA is dissolved in the liquid, so it goes through. Then the DNA is easier to see and is not mixed up with bits of fruit."
- b) No. One DNA molecule is far too thin to see (about 2 nanometres wide), even with a school microscope. Secure: "No. We can only see the white strands because millions of DNA strands have clumped together. One strand on its own is much too thin to see."
- c) Any of: protein, bits of fruit or other cell material, pectin or other carbohydrate from the fruit, a little soap or salt. RNA is also correct, for a strong student.

**Challenge**
- a) Strawberry, because each cell has more sets of chromosomes, so more DNA in every cell. Secure: adds a condition, for example "only if we use the same mass of fruit and the same method", or notes that cell size and water content also matter, so the prediction needs testing.
- b) I change: salt or no salt (or the amount of salt). I measure: the amount of DNA, for example the height of the white layer in mm, or a score from 0 (none) to 3 (lots) using photos. I keep the same: the fruit and how much, the amount of water and washing-up liquid, squashing time, the alcohol (type, amount, how cold), waiting time. Secure: only one thing changes, the measurement is described clearly enough to repeat, and at least three things are kept the same.

**My thinking**
Any thoughtful answer, in either language. Listen for: DNA is a set of instructions. Every living thing uses the same chemical code, but the code is in a different order, so it gives different instructions and builds a different living thing.

**Exit ticket**
1. nucleus 细胞核. 2. does not dissolve 不溶于. 3. Any honest answer: read these to plan what to recap next time.
