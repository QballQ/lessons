# DNA from fruit: quick teacher guide | 从水果中提取DNA：教师速查

Standalone practical, any year group | About 30 minutes hands-on, fits a 40-minute lesson | Groups of 3 or 4
Chinese text status: draft, needs native-speaker check

Printable version: `DNA_extraction_from_fruit.pdf` (page 1 bench sheet for you, page 2 two student step cards to cut in half).

---

## 1. The whole thing in one line

**Squash** the fruit in soapy salty water, **filter** it, **pour cold alcohol on top**, **wait**, and white strands of DNA appear at the line between the layers.

## 2. Kit per group

| Item | Amount | If you do not have it |
|---|---|---|
| Ripe kiwi fruit, peeled (or ripe banana, peeled) | Half a kiwi (or a third of a banana) | Strawberries work best of all if you can get them: 2 or 3, green tops off |
| Extraction liquid (recipe below) | 20 ml (4 teaspoons) | |
| Zip-lock sandwich bag | 1 | Cup and fork, or pestle and mortar |
| Coffee filter | 1 | 2 sheets of kitchen paper, a clean J-cloth, or a tea strainer |
| Funnel | 1 | Top cut off a plastic bottle, or fix the filter over a cup with an elastic band |
| Clear cup to catch the liquid | 1 | Any cup |
| Test tube | 1 | Any narrow, clear glass or plastic tube or small bottle. Narrow is better: the layers are easier to see |
| Ice-cold alcohol | About 10 ml, handed out by you at that step | See Section 4 |
| Wooden skewer or cocktail stick | 1 | Unbent paper clip with a small hook at the end, or a plastic straw |
| Teaspoon, or 10 ml oral syringe (no needle, from a pharmacy) | 1 | |
| Goggles | 1 each | |

**For a class of 8 groups:** 4 kiwis plus 1 spare, 200 ml extraction liquid, about 150 ml alcohol (includes spare).

## 3. Extraction liquid (make on the day, 5 minutes)

For 8 groups (200 ml):

- 180 ml water (tap water is fine)
- 20 ml washing-up liquid (4 teaspoons), clear if you have it
- 6 g salt (1 level teaspoon)

Stir gently until the salt dissolves. Do not whisk it: you want as little froth as possible. Label the bottle **Extraction liquid 提取液: water, washing-up liquid, salt 水、洗洁精、食盐**.

## 4. The cold alcohol: what to use

Pharmacy alcohol is fine. **What matters is the strength (the % on the label): the higher the better.**

| Alcohol | Use it? |
|---|---|
| 95% ethanol (95%酒精), or anhydrous ethanol (无水乙醇) | Best. It is what the Chinese senior-school biology practical "DNA的粗提取与鉴定" uses, so ask the chemistry or biology prep room first. Also sold at some pharmacies and online |
| Isopropyl alcohol 90% or more (异丙醇, "rubbing alcohol") | Very good |
| Methylated spirits or other denatured alcohol | Works. Clear is easier to see through than purple, but the dye does not stop it working |
| 75% medical alcohol (75%医用酒精), the usual Chinese pharmacy alcohol; 70% rubbing alcohol | Works, but gives less. Make it as cold as you can, put only 2 cm of fruit liquid in the tube, use a deeper alcohol layer, and wait the full 5 minutes. **Do a trial run first** |
| Hand sanitiser, gels, anything with glycerine, aloe or perfume, alcohol wipes, iodine antiseptic (碘伏), drinking spirits | No |

**Getting it cold:** pour only what you need (about 150 ml) into a small plastic bottle, cap it tightly, label it, and put it in a freezer for a few hours or overnight. Ethanol and isopropyl will not freeze in a normal freezer. Keep it there for a short time only and keep the cap tight: alcohol vapour in a household freezer is a fire risk. No freezer: stand the bottle in a bowl of ice and water for 30 minutes. In the lesson, keep the bottle standing in a cup of ice. Room-temperature alcohol still works, just less well.

## 5. Method and timings (40-minute lesson)

| Min | Students do | You say (the why) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 | Goggles on. Safety briefing. Hook: "Is there DNA in your breakfast?" | Every living thing has DNA, the instructions for building it. Fruit is made of cells, and every cell has DNA |
| 5 to 9 | Fruit and 20 ml extraction liquid into the bag, squeeze the air out, seal. Squash gently for 2 to 3 minutes until there are no big lumps. Try not to make froth | Squashing breaks open the cell walls |
| 9 to 17 | Pour the mush into the filter over the cup. Let it drip for 5 minutes. Do not squeeze the filter (it pushes pulp through) | Washing-up liquid breaks the oily membranes round the cell and the nucleus, the way it breaks up grease on plates, so the DNA comes out. Salt helps the DNA strands clump together. The filter removes the solid bits: the DNA is in the liquid |
| 17 to 19 | Pour the filtered liquid into the tube until it is a quarter full (2 to 3 cm) | |
| 19 to 21 | You hand out the cold alcohol. Tilt the tube and trickle the alcohol slowly down the inside wall with the spoon or syringe, until the alcohol layer is about twice as deep as the fruit layer. **Do not shake or stir** | Alcohol floats on top because it is less dense than water |
| 21 to 26 | Hold the tube still and watch the line between the layers for 3 to 5 minutes | DNA does not dissolve in alcohol, so it comes out of the liquid as white strands where the two layers meet. Cold makes more come out, and slows the enzymes in the fruit that chop DNA up |
| 26 to 31 | Put the stick in at the line and twist slowly, like winding spaghetti. Lift the white strands out | |
| 31 to 40 | Look closely. Talk about what it is (Section 6). Clean up (Section 9) | |

**Optional, for a better result if you have time:** stand the sealed bag in a bowl of hot tap water (about 60°C, hot but not boiling) for 10 minutes after squashing, then cool it before filtering. You handle the hot water. Skip it in a 40-minute lesson.

**Stretch idea, ties in with fair tests:** each group changes one thing (kiwi or banana, with or without salt, cold or room-temperature alcohol) and compares how much DNA they get.

## 6. What they are looking at

- A clump of **millions of DNA strands** tangled together, with some other cell material mixed in (protein, and pectin from the fruit). It is a rough extract, not pure DNA.
- **You cannot see the double helix.** One DNA molecule is about 2 nanometres wide, far too thin to see even with a school microscope. They see it only because millions of strands have clumped.
- **Why kiwi works well:** kiwi cells have 6 copies of each set of chromosomes, bananas 3, strawberries 8 (that is why strawberries give the most). More copies, more DNA.
- **Wow fact:** the DNA in one human cell, stretched out, would be about 2 metres long.
- **Kiwi fact:** kiwi contains an enzyme (actinidin) that breaks down protein, the reason fresh kiwi stops jelly setting.

**Misconceptions to watch for**

| They think | Put it right |
|---|---|
| "I can see the double helix" | No: one molecule is far too thin. This is millions of strands in a clump |
| "The alcohol makes the DNA" | The DNA was already there. The alcohol only makes it come out of the liquid |
| "Fruit is not alive, so it has no DNA" | DNA is a molecule. It stays in the cells after the fruit is picked |
| "The DNA is only in the seeds" | Every cell has DNA, including the flesh |

## 7. If it does not work

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Nothing appears | Alcohol too weak or too warm; too much water; not enough fruit; not waited long enough | Wait 5 to 10 minutes. Add more cold alcohol gently. Next time: stronger alcohol, colder, less liquid in the tube |
| Cloudy all through, no clear line | Alcohol poured too fast, or the tube was shaken | Leave it still: some DNA will still float up |
| Thick froth on top | Squashed too hard | Leave the froth behind when pouring into the tube |
| Bits of fruit in the tube | Filter squeezed | Let it settle for a minute, then pour off the clear liquid |
| Banana gives a gloopy mess | Banana is starchy and sticky | Use kiwi or strawberry, or use less banana |
| Strands break when lifting | DNA is fragile; stirred instead of twisted | Twist slowly in one direction at the line |

## 8. Safety | 注意安全

**Do a 15-minute trial run with the exact alcohol you bought**, a few days before. This is the most useful prep there is.

- Alcohol is highly flammable. No flames or heaters nearby. You hand out small amounts; keep the lid on the bottle. Open a window.
  酒精易燃。附近不能有明火或加热器。由老师分发少量酒精，酒精瓶要盖好盖子。打开窗户通风。
- Pharmacy and denatured alcohol is poisonous. Never drink or taste it. Nothing in this practical is eaten.
  药店酒精和变性酒精有毒。不要喝，也不要尝。这个实验里的所有东西都不能吃。
- Alcohol and washing-up liquid hurt eyes. Wear goggles. If any gets in an eye, rinse with water for 10 minutes and tell the teacher.
  酒精和洗洁精会伤害眼睛。请戴护目镜。如果溅到眼睛里，用清水冲洗10分钟，并告诉老师。
- Kiwi and banana can cause allergies. Check the allergy list before the lesson. A student with an allergy uses the other fruit, or watches. Do not use latex gloves (latex allergy is linked to kiwi and banana allergy).
  猕猴桃和香蕉可能引起过敏。课前查看过敏名单。过敏的学生换另一种水果，或者只观察。不要用乳胶手套（乳胶过敏和猕猴桃、香蕉过敏有关）。
- Spills: wipe up alcohol with paper towels straight away and open a window.
  洒出的酒精：马上用纸巾擦干，并打开窗户。

## 9. Prep checklist and clean-up

**A few days before**
- [ ] Check the allergy list for kiwi, banana and latex
- [ ] Find or buy the alcohol (Section 4) and do a 15-minute trial run with it
- [ ] Buy the fruit: ripe kiwis (soft when pressed) or ripe bananas (some brown spots)

**The day before**
- [ ] Alcohol into a small, capped, labelled bottle, into the freezer. Label: **Cold alcohol: flammable, do not drink 冷酒精：易燃，不可饮用**

**On the day (about 15 minutes)**
- [ ] Make the extraction liquid (Section 3)
- [ ] Peel and halve the fruit (or students do it with a plastic knife)
- [ ] One tray per group: bag, filter, funnel, cup, tube, stick, spoon, goggles
- [ ] A cup of ice for the alcohol bottle on your desk

**Clean-up**
- Fruit mush and filters: in a bag, in the bin.
- Used liquids: small amounts down the sink with plenty of running water (check your school's rules).
- Leftover clean alcohol: keep it in its labelled bottle in the flammables cupboard. Do not pour it back into the stock bottle.
- Wipe tables. Everyone washes their hands.
- If students want to keep their DNA: a capped tube with a little alcohol, kept in the classroom. It does not go home.

## 11. Student step card | 学生步骤卡

The PDF prints two of these on page 2. Cut in half: one card per group.

1. Put on your goggles. 戴上护目镜。
2. Put the fruit and 4 teaspoons of extraction liquid in the bag. Squeeze out the air and seal it. 把水果和4茶匙提取液放进袋子里。挤出空气，封好袋子。
3. Gently squash the fruit for 2 minutes. Try not to make bubbles. 轻轻挤压水果2分钟。尽量不要产生泡沫。
4. Pour it into the filter. Let it drip for 5 minutes. Do not squeeze. 把它倒进滤纸里。让它滴5分钟。不要挤压。
5. Pour the liquid into the tube until it is a quarter full. 把滤出的液体倒进试管，到四分之一满。
6. Ask your teacher for the cold alcohol. 向老师领取冷酒精。
7. Tilt the tube. Pour the alcohol slowly down the side. Do not shake. 把试管倾斜。沿着管壁慢慢倒入酒精。不要摇晃。
8. Wait 3 to 5 minutes. Watch the line between the two layers. 等3到5分钟。观察两层液体之间的分界线。
9. Twist the stick at the line and lift out the white strands. This is DNA! 在分界线处慢慢转动小棍，把白色的丝挑出来。这就是DNA！
10. Clean up and wash your hands. 清理桌面，洗手。

**Think 想一想**
- Why do we squash the fruit? 为什么要挤压水果？
- What does the washing-up liquid do? 洗洁精有什么作用？
- Why does the DNA appear where the alcohol is? 为什么DNA出现在酒精层？

**Answers (for you):** squashing breaks open the cell walls; washing-up liquid breaks the membranes round the cell and nucleus, so the DNA comes out; DNA does not dissolve in alcohol, so it comes out of the liquid where the alcohol meets it.
